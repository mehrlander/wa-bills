State of Washington      57th Legislature     2001 Regular Session

Read first time 01/19/2001.  Referred to Committee on Transportation.

_1      AN ACT Relating to a tax exemption for materials and labor used

_2  on public roads, streets, and highways; and adding a new section

_3  to chapter 82.08 RCW.

     

_4  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_5      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  A new section is added to chapter 82.08

_6  RCW to read as follows:

_7      The taxes levied by RCW 82.08.020 do not apply to materials and

_8  labor used in the construction or maintenance of publicly owned

_9  roads, streets, or highways.
