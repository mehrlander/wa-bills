     AN ACT Relating to the management of hazardous wastes in Puget Sound; creating a new section; and providing an expiration date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   (1) The department of ecology shall, as part of its participation in the previously scheduled annual review of the dredged material management program, initiate a stakeholder discussion to review and consider changes to the existing dredge management program as it relates to polychlorinated biphenyls.
     (2) The stakeholder discussion must include an initial review of the existing sampling and management protocol for polychlorinated biphenyls in dredge materials, including an examination of the current chronic sublethal sediment bioassay and other protocols to determine the chronic effects of contaminates on marine life, and develop a time frame for updating the management protocol to reflect the most recent advancements in technology and science.
     (3) The department of ecology shall present a summary of the discussion, the anticipated next steps forward, and the identified time frame to the appropriate committees of the legislature by September 1, 2008.
     (4) This section expires July 30, 2009.
