     AN ACT Relating to minimum notification requirements for property and casualty insurance policy changes; adding a new section to chapter 48.22 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 48.27 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 48.22 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) All insurers doing business in this state shall provide a notice of policy change to each producer receiving an insurance policy from the insurer under the following circumstances:
     (a) The insurance policy covers personal lines or business lines of casualty insurance, not including medical, workers' compensation, employers' liability, nuclear liability, or disability insurance; and
     (b) The insurance policy contains a material change from either the last previous standard insurance policy that the insurer provided to the producer for sale to the public or a material change from the coverage last previously provided under a policy being renewed.
     (2) The notice of policy change must include the change or changes in coverage, described in plain language on a separate page of paper prefacing the changed policy or policy being renewed.
     (3) Each producer shall communicate the contents of the notice of policy change to the consumer prior to the consumer's acceptance of the policy or policy being renewed.
     (4) Any policy change not made in compliance with this section is not effective and is unenforceable.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 48.27 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) All insurers doing business in this state shall provide a notice of policy change to each producer receiving an insurance policy from the insurer under the following circumstances:
     (a) The insurance policy covers personal lines or business lines of property insurance; and
     (b) The insurance policy contains a material change from either the last previous standard insurance policy that the insurer provided to the producer for sale to the public or a material change from the coverage last previously provided under a policy being renewed.
     (2) The notice of policy change must include the change or changes in coverage, described in plain language on a separate page of paper prefacing the changed standard policy or policy being renewed.
     (3) Each producer shall communicate the contents of the notice of policy change to the purchaser prior to the purchaser's acceptance of the policy or policy being renewed.
     (4) Any policy change not made in compliance with this section is not effective and is unenforceable.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act takes effect July 1, 2011, and applies to insurance policies covered by this act that are issued or renewed on or after July 1, 2011.
