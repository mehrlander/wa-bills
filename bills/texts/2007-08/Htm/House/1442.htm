     AN ACT Relating to private residential fire sprinkler water charges; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The state building code council shall develop a model pricing plan for private residential fire sprinkler water charges by December 1, 2007. The council must survey other states during the development process and make the supporting data available to local jurisdictions upon request.
