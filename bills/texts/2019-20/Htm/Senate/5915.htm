AN ACT Relating to creating greater flexibility for cities and counties to plan for the future of their citizens by minimizing costs; amending RCW 36.70A.040; creating a new section; and repealing RCW 36.70A.540, 36.70A.250, 36.70A.252, 36.70A.260, 36.70A.270, 36.70A.280, 36.70A.290, 36.70A.295, 36.70A.300, 36.70A.302, 36.70A.305, 36.70A.310, 36.70A.320, 36.70A.3201, 36.70A.330, 36.70A.335, and 36.70A.340.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. The legislature finds that the comprehensive planning efforts made by the growth management act have resulted in high population densities in the most populous county in the state and a dearth of economic growth in other areas of the state. In order to provide for more equitable distribution of economic opportunity throughout the state, the legislature finds that it is important to relieve those communities of mandatory planning obligations. The legislature finds that it is important for communities across the state to have access to a planning framework when it is appropriate. The legislature also finds that that access should be voluntary and not mandatory.
