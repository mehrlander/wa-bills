Providing relief for crop damage.

    AN ACT Relating to funding crop damage; reenacting and amending RCW 43.84.092 and 43.84.092; creating new sections; making an appropriation; providing an effective date; providing an expiration date; and declaring an emergency.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  The legislature finds that the agricultural industry is vital to the economy of the state and the welfare of its citizens.  The legislature further finds that catastrophic natural events such as storms, fires, and floods can place the viability of significant segments of the agricultural industry at risk, and that such events jeopardize the economic well-being of the state and the public welfare.  The legislature accordingly declares that providing disaster relief to help restore significant segments of the agricultural industry having suffered catastrophic loss from the storm that occurred on June 26 and 27, 2001, is an essential public purpose and public function.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  (1) The crop damage management account is created in the custody of the state treasurer.  Amounts appropriated from the Nisqually earthquake account will be deposited in the account.  Expenditures from the account may be used only for damage caused by the severe storm, a natural disaster, that occurred on June 26 and 27, 2001, which damaged crops in thirteen counties designated by the governor and the secretary of the United States department of agriculture.  Only the director of the department of agriculture or the director's designee may authorize expenditures from the account.  The account is subject to allotment procedures under chapter 43.88 RCW, but an appropriation is not required for expenditures.

    (2) The department of agriculture shall develop criteria for distributing amounts appropriated to the account to affected farmers in the form of limited financial assistance.  The criteria shall designate a minimum of three levels of limited financial assistance, based on the severity of actual damages.  The department of agriculture shall consult with affected farmer groups in developing the levels of limited financial assistance.

    (3) The department of agriculture is exempt from the requirements of chapter 19.85 RCW for any rule adopted to administer this limited financial assistance program.

    (4) To be eligible for limited financial assistance, a farmer must first apply for and qualify for an emergency loan from the farm service agency of the United States department of agriculture.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  The sum of seven million dollars, or as much thereof as may be necessary, is appropriated for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2002, from the Nisqually earthquake account to the crop damage management account for the purposes specified under section 1 of this act.

    (3) Except for the provisions of RCW 43.84.160, the treasury income account may be utilized for the payment of purchased banking services on behalf of treasury funds including, but not limited to, depository, safekeeping, and disbursement functions for the state treasury and affected state agencies.  The treasury income account is subject in all respects to chapter 43.88 RCW, but no appropriation is required for payments to financial institutions.  Payments shall occur prior to distribution of earnings set forth in subsection (4) of this section.
