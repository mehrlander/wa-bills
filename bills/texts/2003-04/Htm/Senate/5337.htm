     AN ACT Relating to the agency council on coordinated transportation; amending RCW 47.06B.901; and repealing RCW 47.06B.030 and 47.06B.900.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 47.06B.030 (Council -- Duties) and 1999 c 385 s 5, 1999 c 372 s 13, & 1998 c 173 s 3; and
     (2) RCW 47.06B.900 (Council--Termination) and 1999 c 385 s 7 & 1998 c 173 s 6.

Sec. 2   RCW 47.06B.901 and 1999 c 385 s 8 are each amended to read as follows:
     The following acts or parts of acts, as now existing or hereafter amended, are each repealed, effective June 30, 2008:
     (1) RCW 47.06B.010 and 1999 c 385 § 1 & 1998 c 173 § 1;
     (2) RCW 47.06B.012 and 1999 c 385 § 2;
     (3) RCW 47.06B.015 and 1999 c 385 § 3;
     (4) RCW 47.06B.020 and ((1999 c 385 § 4 &)) 1998 c 173 § 2; and
     (5) ((RCW 47.06B.030 and 1999 c 385 § 5 & 1998 c 173 § 3; and
     (6))) RCW 47.06B.040 and 1999 c 385 § 6.
