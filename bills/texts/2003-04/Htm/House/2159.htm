     AN ACT Relating to information an employer must disclose to an applicant for employment; and adding a new section to chapter 49.46 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 49.46 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) An employer shall provide the following information to an applicant for a position of employment:
     (a) The nature of the work;
     (b) The hours of work expected each week;
     (c) The wage rate paid for each hour;
     (d) Any benefits provided, including but not limited to health insurance, sick leave, vacation leave, and child care;
     (e) The conditions of work, including but not limited to available shifts and meal and rest breaks;
     (f) Any required alcohol or controlled substances testing; and
     (g) Any required uniforms as defined in RCW 49.12.450 or other required apparel.
     (2) The employer must provide the information specified in subsection (1) of this section to the applicant in writing, and within twenty-four hours of receiving his or her application for the position of employment.
     (3) For the purposes of this section:
     (a) "Employer" means a person who had ten or more full-time equivalent employees in the previous year; and
     (b) "Position" means a position of employment paying less than one hundred fifty percent of the minimum wage rate calculated under RCW 49.46.020.
     (4) The legislature declares that the public policies articulated in this section depend on the procedures established in this section and no civil or criminal action may be maintained relying on the public policies articulated in this section without complying with the procedures set forth in this section, and to that end all civil actions and civil causes of action for such injuries and all jurisdiction of the courts of this state over such causes are hereby abolished, except as provided in this section.
