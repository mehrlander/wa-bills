     AN ACT Relating to transferring funds from the budget stabilization account to the general fund; and making appropriations.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   (1) The state treasurer shall transfer one hundred forty million ninety-eight thousand dollars or as much as is available from the budget stabilization account to the state general fund for fiscal year 2012.
     (2) The state treasurer shall transfer one hundred forty-nine million four hundred eighty-four thousand dollars or as much as is available from the budget stabilization account to the state general fund for fiscal year 2013.
