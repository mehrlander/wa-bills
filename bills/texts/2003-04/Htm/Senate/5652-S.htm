     AN ACT Relating to adoption-related advertising; and amending RCW 26.33.410.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 26.33.410 and 1989 c 255 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:
     Nothing in RCW 26.33.400 applies to any radio or television station or any publisher, printer, or distributor of any newspaper, magazine, billboard, or other advertising medium which accepts advertising in good faith without knowledge of its violation of any provision of RCW 26.33.400. Publishers of annual and semiannual directories must attempt to verify compliance with RCW 26.33.400.
