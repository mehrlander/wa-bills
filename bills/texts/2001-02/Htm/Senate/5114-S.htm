READ FIRST TIME 02/09/01. 

_1      AN ACT Relating to motorcycles; amending RCW 46.68.065; adding

_2  a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; and adding a new section to

_3  chapter 82.12 RCW.

     

_4  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_5      Sec. 1.  RCW 46.68.065 and 1982 c 77 s 8 are each amended to read

_6  as follows:

_7      There is hereby created the motorcycle safety education account

_8  in the highway safety fund of the state treasury, to the credit of

_9  which shall be deposited all moneys directed by law to be credited

10  thereto.  All expenses incurred by the director of the department of

11  licensing in administering RCW 46.20.505 through 46.20.520 shall

12  be borne by appropriations from this account, and moneys deposited

13  into this account shall be used only for the purposes authorized

14  in RCW 46.20.505 through 46.20.520.

     

15      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  A new section is added to chapter 82.08

16  RCW to read as follows:

 

                               p. 1                      SSB 5114

_1      The tax levied by RCW 82.08.020 does not apply to sales of

_2  motorcycles purchased for use in a motorcycle operator training

_3  and education program created under RCW 46.20.520.

     

_4      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  A new section is added to chapter 82.12

_5  RCW to read as follows:

_6      The provisions of this chapter do not apply with respect to the

_7  use of motorcycles loaned or purchased for use in a motorcycle

_8  operator training and education program created under RCW

_9  46.20.520.
