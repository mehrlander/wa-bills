     AN ACT Relating to making adjustments in the unemployment insurance system to enhance benefit and tax equity; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that it is in the best interest of unemployed workers, businesses, and the state to maintain a stable and solvent unemployment insurance system. The legislature intends to make adjustments to benefit and tax equity that ensure both the stability and solvency of the system.
