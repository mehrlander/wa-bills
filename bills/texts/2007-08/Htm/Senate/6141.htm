     AN ACT Relating to forest health; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that Washington currently faces forest health threats. In response to these threats, the legislature intends to create a comprehensive forest health program for the state.
