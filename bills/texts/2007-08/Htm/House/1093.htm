NEW SECTION.  Sec. 202   A new section is added to 2005 c 313 (uncodified) to read as follows:
     FOR THE WASHINGTON STATE PATROL. The appropriations to the Washington state patrol in chapter 370, Laws of 2006 shall be expended for the programs and in the amounts specified herein.
