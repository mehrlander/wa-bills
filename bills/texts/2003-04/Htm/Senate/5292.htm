     AN ACT Relating to frequent user coupons for the state ferry system; adding a new section to chapter 47.60 RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that:
     (1) Frequent users of the state's ferry system often purchase frequent user coupons. Currently, coupon books have an expiration date of ninety days after the date of purchase;
     (2) Ferry fares have been raised considerably over the last year; and
     (3) Citizens who cannot use all of their frequent user coupons lose significant money each year.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 47.60 RCW to read as follows:
     Frequent user coupons for the Washington state ferry system are valid for one year from the date of purchase, after which time the tickets will not be accepted for passage. Unused coupons are eligible for refund up to one year from the date of purchase.
