     AN ACT Relating to designating the honor and remember flag as Washington's emblem of the service and sacrifice by our military personnel; adding a new section to chapter 1.20 RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   This act may be known and cited as the honor and remember flag act.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 1.20 RCW to read as follows:
     The honor and remember flag created by Honor and Remember, Inc., is designated as the official Washington state emblem of the service and sacrifice by the brave men and women of the United States armed forces who have given their lives in the line of duty.
