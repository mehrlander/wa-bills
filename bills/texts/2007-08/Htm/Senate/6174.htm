     AN ACT Relating to anatomical gift statements; and amending RCW 46.20.113.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 46.20.113 and 1993 c 228 s 18 are each amended to read as follows:
     (1) The department of licensing shall provide a statement whereby the licensee may certify his or her ((willingness)) unwillingness to make an anatomical gift under RCW 68.50.540((, as now or hereafter amended)). The department shall provide the statement in at least one of the following ways:
     (((1))) (a) On each driver's license; or
     (((2))) (b) With each driver's license; or
     (((3))) (c) With each in-person driver's license application.
     (2) A licensee who fails to certify an unwillingness to make an anatomical gift under this section consents to make an anatomical gift under RCW 68.50.540.
