     AN ACT Relating to excluding car-sharing activities from the rental car tax; amending RCW 82.08.011; creating a new section; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that car sharing differs from traditional car rental in important ways that benefit the state; that while offering a practical alternative to car ownership, car sharing reduces vehicle ownership, reduces vehicle miles traveled, and increases the use of public transit. The collective impact translates to reduced congestion on roads and reduced greenhouse gas forming carbon emissions in the air. The legislature intends, through this legislation, to continue to support car-sharing activities by exempting such limited activities from the rental car tax.

Sec. 2   RCW 82.08.011 and 1992 c 194 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:
     (1) Except as otherwise provided in subsection (2) of this section, for purposes of this chapter, "retail car rental" means renting a rental car, as defined in RCW 46.04.465, to a consumer.
     (2) For the purposes of this section, "retail car rental" does not include car sharing as defined in RCW 82.70.010 where: (a) Passenger vehicles are provided to members of an organization; (b) the organization charges a membership fee separate from the charge for the use of a specific vehicle; (c) the average of all paid usage periods during the previous calendar year was less than eight hours; (d) vehicles are available to members in parking spaces at dispersed locations or facilities, which locations or facilities are not owned or controlled by the car-sharing business; and in the previous calendar year, not more than fifty percent of the locations or facilities had more than four vehicles available to members at any one time; and (e) no separate written agreement is required each time a member reserves and uses a vehicle.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act takes effect July 1, 2008.
