     AN ACT Relating to ensuring the safety of milk and dairy products; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that the protection of the public from the sale or distribution of hazardous milk and dairy products requires strong enforcement mechanisms available to regulatory agencies. The legislature must ensure that the agencies charged with protecting the public are able to respond to unsafe practices rapidly, comprehensively, and conclusively.
