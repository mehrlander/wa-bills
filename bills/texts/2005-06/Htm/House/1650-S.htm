     AN ACT Relating to citations and infractions; and adding a new section to chapter 46.63 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 46.63 RCW to read as follows:
     Whenever any person is served with a notice of infraction or citation, whether traffic or civil, by electronic means or by mail, the signature provisions of RCW 46.61.021, 46.63.060, 46.64.025, 7.80.070, 7.80.160, 7.84.050, 18.27.240, 18.106.190, 20.01.482, 43.63B.140, and 81.112.230 shall not apply.
