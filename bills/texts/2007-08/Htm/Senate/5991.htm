     AN ACT Relating to motor fuel retailers; and adding a new section to chapter 19.120 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 19.120 RCW to read as follows:
     A motor fuel retailer shall not sell motor fuel at a price that includes a fraction of one cent.
