     AN ACT Relating to education requirements for family day care providers; and adding a new section to chapter 43.215 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 43.215 RCW to read as follows:
     A family day care provider may meet the minimum education requirement for licensing by achieving a level three rating in the early achievers program, Washington state's quality rating improvement system.
