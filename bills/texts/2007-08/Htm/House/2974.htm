     AN ACT Relating to the study of the Pledge of Allegiance; and amending RCW 28A.230.170.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 28A.230.170 and 2006 c 263 s 403 are each amended to read as follows:
     The study of the Constitution of the United States, the Pledge of Allegiance, and the Constitution of the state of Washington shall be a condition prerequisite to graduation from the public and private high schools of this state. The superintendent of public instruction shall provide by rule for the implementation of this section.
