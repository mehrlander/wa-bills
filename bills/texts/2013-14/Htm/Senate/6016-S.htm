     AN ACT Relating to the grace period for enrollees of the Washington health benefit exchange; and adding a new section to chapter 43.71 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 43.71 RCW to read as follows:
     The exchange must provide electronic notification to the qualified health plan before the sixth of the month indicating an enrollee has not paid the premium.
