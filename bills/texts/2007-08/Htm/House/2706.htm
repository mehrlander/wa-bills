     AN ACT Relating to providing notice in real estate transactions of proximity to lands used for public hunting; amending RCW 64.06.022; and adding a new section to chapter 77.12 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 77.12 RCW to read as follows:
     The department may, when deemed appropriate by the director based on local circumstances and departmental resources, partner with volunteer organizations and real estate professionals to educate potential real estate sellers and buyers of the location and traditional uses of any land in proximity to a potential transaction where public hunting has been, is, or may be permitted.

Sec. 2   RCW 64.06.022 and 2006 c 77 s 1 are each amended to read as follows:
     A seller of real property shall make available to the buyer the following ((statement)) two statements regarding possible preexisting nearby land uses:
     (1) "This notice is to inform you that the real property you are considering for purchase may lie in close proximity to a farm. The operation of a farm involves usual and customary agricultural practices, which are protected under RCW 7.48.305, the Washington right to farm act"; and
     (2) "This notice is to inform you that the real property you are considering for purchase may lie in close proximity to land that has been traditionally open for public hunting, an activity which may include the use of firearms. Hunting is a lawful and accepted activity when conducted at a time and place, and in a manner, consistent with regulations adopted by the fish and wildlife commission pursuant to Title 77 RCW."
