66th Legislature

Prefiled 12/10/19.Read first time 01/13/20.Referred to Committee on Health & Long Term Care.
AN ACT Relating to direct primary care oversight; adding a new chapter to Title 18 RCW; recodifying RCW 48.150.005, 48.150.010, 48.150.020, 48.150.030, 48.150.040, 48.150.050, 48.150.060, 48.150.070, 48.150.080, 48.150.090, and 48.150.110; and repealing RCW 48.150.100.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. RCW 48.150.005, 48.150.010, 48.150.020, 48.150.030, 48.150.040, 48.150.050, 48.150.060, 48.150.070, 48.150.080, 48.150.090, and 48.150.110 are each recodified as sections in a new chapter in Title 18 RCW.
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2. RCW 48.150.100 (Annual statements—Commissioner's report) and 2007 c 267 s 12 are each repealed.
