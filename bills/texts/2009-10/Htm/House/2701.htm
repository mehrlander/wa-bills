     AN ACT Relating to the time of commencement of regular legislative sessions; and amending RCW 44.04.010.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 44.04.010 and 1980 c 87 s 27 are each amended to read as follows:
     Regular sessions of the legislature shall be held annually, commencing at 12:00 p.m. on the second Monday of January.
