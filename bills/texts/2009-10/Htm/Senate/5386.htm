     AN ACT Relating to electronic recording of court proceedings; amending RCW 3.02.030 and 3.02.040; and adding new sections to chapter 2.08 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 3.02.030 and 1980 c 162 s 3 are each amended to read as follows:
     ((The supreme court may, by court rule, establish a method of making a record of the proceedings of a)) All courts of limited jurisdiction must use electronic recording equipment to record oral testimony and other oral proceedings for purposes of review.

Sec. 2   RCW 3.02.040 and 1980 c 162 s 4 are each amended to read as follows:
     The administrator for the courts shall supervise the selection, installation, and operation of ((any)) the electronic recording equipment used in courts of limited jurisdiction.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 2.08 RCW to read as follows:
     All of the superior courts must use electronic recording equipment to record oral testimony and other oral proceedings for purposes of review.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   A new section is added to chapter 2.08 RCW to read as follows:
     The administrator for the courts shall supervise the selection, installation, and operation of the electronic recording equipment used in the superior courts.
