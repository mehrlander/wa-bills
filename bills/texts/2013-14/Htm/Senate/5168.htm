     AN ACT Relating to removal of the insurance premium tax credit under the Washington insurance guaranty association act; and repealing RCW 48.32.145.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   RCW 48.32.145 (Credit against premium tax for assessments paid pursuant to RCW 48.32.060(1)(c)) and 1997 c 300 s 1, 1993 sp.s. c 25 s 901, 1977 ex.s. c 183 s 1, & 1975-'76 2nd ex.s. c 109 s 11 are each repealed.
