     AN ACT Relating to the value of benefits provided to any executive officer, employee, or director of a public service company; and adding a new section to chapter 80.04 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 80.04 RCW to read as follows:
     The combined value of all cash and noncash benefits provided to any executive officer, employee, or director of a public service company that exceeds the annual salary of the governor of the state of Washington shall not be recovered by the company in its rates or charges.
