     AN ACT Relating to eliminating the mandatory retirement provision for district judges; and repealing RCW 3.74.030.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   RCW 3.74.030 (Mandatory retirement for district judges) and 1984 c 258 s 56 & 1969 ex.s. c 6 s 1 are each repealed.
