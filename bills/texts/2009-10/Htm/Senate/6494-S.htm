     AN ACT Relating to the state board of health rules that impact school districts; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   By June 30, 2011, the department of health and the state board of health shall revise their rules pertaining to primary and secondary school facilities to eliminate rules that are obsolete or not specifically funded or implemented.
