     AN ACT Relating to lead blood level assessments; and adding a new section to chapter 74.09 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 74.09 RCW to read as follows:
     The department shall give a high priority to implementing the requirement to conduct lead blood level assessments under this chapter for any eligible children younger than twenty-one years old in accordance with early and periodic screening and diagnostic treatment services as defined in section 1905 of Title XIX of the federal social security act and its implementing regulations and guidelines.
