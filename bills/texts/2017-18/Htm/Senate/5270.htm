AN ACT Relating to expiration dates affecting the department of natural resources' contract harvesting program; amending 2013 c 255 s 1 and 2009 c 418 s 7 (uncodified); repealing 2010 c 126 s 12; and repealing 2013 c 255 ss 2 and 3 and 2010 c 126 ss 15 and 16 (uncodified).
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
Sec. 1.  2013 c 255 s 1 and 2009 c 418 s 7 (uncodified) are each amended to read as follows:
Section 5 of this act expires January 1, 2019.
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
(1) 2013 c 255 s 2 and 2010 c 126 s 15 (uncodified);
(2) 2013 c 255 s 3 and 2010 c 126 s 16 (uncodified); and
(3) 2010 c 126 s 12.
