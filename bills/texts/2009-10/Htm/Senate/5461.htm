     AN ACT Relating to reserve account and study requirements for condominium associations; and adding a new section to chapter 64.34 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 64.34 RCW to read as follows:
     A condominium association that has ten or less units and limited common areas is not required to follow the requirements under RCW 64.34.380 through 64.34.390.
