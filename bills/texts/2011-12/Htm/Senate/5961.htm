     AN ACT Relating to providing local government funding of tourism promotion, workforce housing, art and heritage programs, and community development; amending RCW 82.14.049, 82.14.360, 36.38.010, 36.100.220, and 67.28.180; and adding a new section to chapter 67.28 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 82.14.049 and 2011 c 174 s 107 are each amended to read as follows:
     (1) The legislative authority of any county may impose a sales and use tax, in addition to the tax authorized by RCW 82.14.030, upon retail car rentals within the county that are taxable by the state under chapters 82.08 and 82.12 RCW. The rate of tax is one percent of the selling price in the case of a sales tax or rental value of the vehicle in the case of a use tax. Proceeds of the tax may not be used to subsidize any professional sports team and must be used solely for the following purposes:
     (a) Acquiring, constructing, maintaining, or operating public sports stadium facilities;
     (b) Engineering, planning, financial, legal, or professional services incidental to public sports stadium facilities;
     (c) Youth or amateur sport activities or facilities; ((or))
     (d) Debt or refinancing debt issued for the purposes of (a) of this subsection (((1) of this section)); or
     (e) For deposit into a special MLK workforce housing, arts and preservation, tourism, convention and trade center, and community development fund as provided in section 3 of this act.
     (2) In a county of one million five hundred thousand or more, at least seventy-five percent of the tax imposed under this section must be used to retire the debt on the stadium under RCW 67.28.180(2)(b)(ii), until that debt is fully retired, and at least seventy-five percent must be deposited as provided under subsection (1)(e) of this section after the debt is fully retired.
     (3) A county with a population of one million five hundred thousand or more may not impose the tax authorized under this section beginning on the first day of the second month following the date on which: (a) The county does not impose both taxes authorized under RCW 82.14.360 through December 31, 2015; or (b) the distribution described under section 6(3)(d) of this act is repealed, modified, or otherwise not in law.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 67.28 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) As provided in subsection (2) of this section, revenue deposited in a special MLK workforce housing, arts and preservation, tourism, convention and trade center, and community development fund, as provided in RCW 67.28.180(3)(d), 82.14.049, and 82.14.360, must be used only for affordable workforce housing; the expansion, construction, repair, or maintenance of a convention and trade center; art museums, cultural museums, heritage museums, and heritage and preservation programs; the arts; the performing arts; other capital or operating programs that promote tourism and attract tourists to the county; and community development.
     (2) Beginning in calendar year 2012, the county must distribute money in the account annually as follows:
     (a)(i) Through calendar year 2012, a minimum of one million dollars for art museums, cultural museums, heritage museums, heritage and preservation programs, the arts, and the performing arts;
     (ii) Beginning January 1, 2013, through December 31, 2016, a minimum of two million dollars must be used for the purposes of (a)(i) of this subsection;
     (iii) Beginning January 1, 2017, through December 31, 2020, a minimum of two million five hundred thousand dollars must be used for the purposes of (a)(i) of this subsection;
     (iv) Beginning with calendar year 2021, 32.5 percent of the revenues described under RCW 67.28.180(3)(d) must be used for the purposes of (a)(i) of this subsection;
     (v) The funds distributed in (a)(i) through (iv) of this subsection should be broadly and equitably distributed throughout the county, and provide support to underserved communities;
     (b)(i) Through calendar year 2012, a minimum of one million dollars for distributions to nonprofit organizations or public housing authorities for affordable workforce housing within one-half of a mile of a transit station, as described under RCW 9.91.025;
     (ii) Beginning January 1, 2013, through December 31, 2016, a minimum of four million dollars must be used for the purposes of (b)(i) of this subsection;
     (iii) Beginning January 1, 2017, through December 31, 2020, a minimum of three million five hundred thousand dollars must be used for the purposes of (b)(i) of this subsection;
     (iv) Beginning with calendar year 2021, twenty-five percent of the revenues described under RCW 67.28.180(3)(d) must be used for the purposes of (b)(i) of this subsection;
     (v) At least forty percent of the funds in this subsection (2)(b) must be used for the purposes of (b)(i) of this subsection in cities with a population of less than six hundred thousand and at least forty percent of the funds must be used for purposes of (b)(i) of this subsection in a city with a population greater than six hundred thousand; and
     (v) At least five percent of the funds used in this subsection (2)(b) must be used for services for homeless youth within the county;
     (c)(i) Beginning January 1, 2012, through December 31, 2015, one million dollars must be divided equally between (c)(ii)(A) and (c)(ii)(B) of this subsection.
     (ii) Beginning January 1, 2016, through December 31, 2023, five hundred thousand dollars must be divided equally:
     (A) For the Pioneer Square-International District community preservation and development authority under RCW 43.167.060 for projects requested by the community preservation and development authority and approved by the county legislative authority. The purpose of the projects must be for historic preservation, economic revitalization, or capital or operating programs that promote tourism and attract tourists to the county. The county must contract with the Pioneer Square-International District community preservation and development authority to perform these services; and
     (B) For Seattle Southside Visitor Services for programs that promote tourism and attract tourists to the county. The county must contract with Seattle Southside Visitor Services to perform these services; and
     (d) The remaining distributions must be for:
     (i) An amount that supports a minimum of one hundred forty million dollars to the construction, expansion, or repair of property owned by a public facilities district created under chapter 36.100 RCW to operate a convention and trade center transferred from a public nonprofit corporation under RCW 36.100.230(1); and
     (ii) Capital or operating programs that promote tourism and attract tourists to the county.
     (3) For the purposes of this section, the following definitions apply:
     (a) "Affordable workforce housing" means housing for a single person, family, or unrelated persons living together whose income is between thirty percent and eighty percent of the median income, adjusted for household size, for the county where the housing is located.
     (b) "Consumer price index" means the Seattle-Tacoma-Bremerton consumer price index for all urban consumers (CPI-U) available from the bureau of labor statistics of the United States department of labor.
     (4) Money deposited in a special MLK workforce housing, arts and preservation, tourism, convention and trade center, and community development fund under this section may not be used to acquire or construct a new stadium facility used by a professional sports franchise or to acquire, construct, repair, or improve a stadium used primarily by a state university.
