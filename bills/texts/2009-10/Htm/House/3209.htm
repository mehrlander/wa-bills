     AN ACT Relating to managing costs of the ferry system; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   It is the intent of the legislature to address cost drivers and revenue projections that impact the ferry system. The legislature, therefore, intends to evaluate and develop strategies to manage costs and improve efficiencies of the ferry system.
