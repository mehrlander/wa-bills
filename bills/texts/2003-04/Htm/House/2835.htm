     AN ACT Relating to noneconomic damage awards in actions against health care providers under chapter 7.70 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 7.70 RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that there is a crisis in the availability and affordability of professional health care liability insurance coverage. The legislature finds that this malpractice insurance crisis threatens the current and future availability and affordability of health care services for the people of the state of Washington by preventing and discouraging health care providers from initiating or continuing their practice in this state. The legislature further finds that an informed jury may consider that a reasonable limitation on the amount of noneconomic damages that may be awarded in actions against health care providers is necessary to ensure the continued availability and affordability of health care by containing the increasing costs of malpractice insurance for health care providers.
     Recognizing the authority of the trier of fact in a civil case to determine the issue of damages, the legislature intends by this act to establish an advisory limit on the amount of noneconomic damages that should be awarded in actions against health care providers. The legislature intends that the trier of fact be informed of the limitation on noneconomic damages established in this act and the fact that the limitation on noneconomic damages is advisory and not binding on the trier of fact.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 7.70 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The limitation on noneconomic damages contained in subsection (2) of this section is advisory and not binding on the trier of fact.
     (2)(a) In an action for damages for injury or death occurring as a result of the provision of health care, the amount of noneconomic damages awarded to the claimant should not exceed four hundred thousand dollars. July 1st of each year, beginning July 1, 2005, the administrator for the courts shall adjust the amount specified in this subsection for inflation in accordance with the method established in RCW 2.12.037. The administrator for the courts shall publish the adjusted figure in the Washington state register.
     (b) The limitation on noneconomic damages in (a) of this subsection should not apply in an action in which the acts or omissions of a defendant were reckless, willful, or wanton.
     (c) In an action involving more than one defendant where at least one of the defendant's acts or omissions was reckless, willful, or wanton and the noneconomic damage award exceeds four hundred thousand dollars as provided under (b) of this subsection, the responsibility for damages of a defendant whose acts or omissions were not reckless, willful, or wanton should be determined as though that defendant was responsible for his or her proportionate share of a noneconomic damage award of four hundred thousand dollars.
     (3) The supreme court shall adopt rules governing the process in which the jury is informed of the advisory limitation on noneconomic damages under this section.
