SENATE BILL 6517
State of Washington
65th Legislature

By Senator Darneille; by request of Department of Corrections
AN ACT Relating to eliminating the sunset provision associated with the drug offense sentencing grid; repealing 2015 c 291 s 9; and repealing 2015 c 291 ss 15 and 16 and 2013 2nd sp.s. c 14 s 10 (uncodified).
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
(1) 2015 c 291 s 9;
(2) 2015 c 291 s 15 (uncodified);
(3) 2015 c 291 s 16 (uncodified); and
(4) 2013 2nd sp.s. c 14 s 10 (uncodified).
