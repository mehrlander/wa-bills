AN ACT Relating to obtaining required clinical experience for licensed practical nurses who complete a nontraditional registered nurse program; and repealing RCW 18.79.380.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  RCW 18.79.380 (Licensed practical nurse/nontraditional registered nurse program—Obtaining required clinical experience) and 2004 c 262 s 7 are each repealed.
