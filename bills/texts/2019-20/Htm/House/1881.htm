State of Washington
66th Legislature

ByRepresentatives Ybarra, Dent, Orcutt, Klippert, and Van Werven
Read first time 02/04/19.Referred to Committee on Finance.
AN ACT Relating to making the custom farming and hauling farm products tax exemptions permanent; repealing 2014 c 140 s 40 and 2007 c 334 s 4 (uncodified); and creating a new section.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
(1) 2014 c 140 s 40 (uncodified); and
(2) 2007 c 334 s 4 (uncodified).
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2. The provisions of RCW 82.32.805 and 82.32.808 do not apply to this act.
