     AN ACT Relating to offering health benefit subsidies for certain retired public employees; and adding a new section to chapter 41.05 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 41.05 RCW to read as follows:
     From September 1, 2012, through August 31, 2015, the health care authority shall provide a subsidy of two hundred fifty dollars per month for health benefit premiums to any member of the teachers' retirement system or the public employees' retirement system who:
     (1) Is ineligible for medicare;
     (2) Applies for retirement under chapter 41.32 or 41.40 RCW between June 1, 2012, and August 31, 2012;
     (3) No later than October 31, 2012, receives the first installment of a retirement allowance as provided under chapter 41.32 or 41.40 RCW; and
     (4) Elects to continue participation in a health insurance plan provided by the board immediately upon retirement.
