     AN ACT Relating to studying state policies on controlled substances; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The Washington state institute for public policy is directed to investigate and report to the legislature by January 1, 2007, on the fiscal and other costs and benefits to the various governments and people of the state of Washington from the implementation of current Washington state policies on controlled substances, excluding alcohol, tobacco, and pharmaceuticals.
