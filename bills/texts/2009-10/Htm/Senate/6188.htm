     AN ACT Relating to the imposition of a minimum bail bond premium fee of ten percent; adding new sections to chapter 18.185 RCW; creating a new section; and prescribing penalties.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature recognizes that corporate surety bail bond premium fees that fall below ten percent of the penalty amount of the bond to be posted encourage flight and thus make it more likely that a defendant will forfeit the bond and fail to appear in court at the designated time. Accordingly, the legislature intends to create uniformity of law and promote public safety by creating a standard bail bond premium fee throughout the state.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A bail bond agent shall charge and collect for his or her premium, commission, or fee an amount of ten percent of the amount of the corporate surety bail bond posted by him or her.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   (1) The director may impose a civil penalty for a violation of this chapter. The civil penalty shall consist of:
     (a) For a first violation, a written warning and a monetary penalty of one hundred dollars;
     (b) For a second violation, mandatory suspension of the bail bond agent's license for a maximum of one year and a monetary penalty of one thousand dollars;
     (c) For a third violation, mandatory revocation of the bail bond agent's license and a monetary penalty of five thousand dollars.
     (2) At the request of the director, the attorney general may bring an action in the name of the state seeking:
     (a) Injunctive relief to prevent the bail bond agent from further violation of this chapter;
     (b) To recover civil penalties imposed under subsection (1) of this section; or
     (c) To recover attorneys' fees and other enforcement costs and disbursements.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   All revenues derived from any civil penalties assessed under section 3 of this act shall be deposited in the business and professions account, pursuant to RCW 43.24.150(1)(g).

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 5   Sections 2 through 4 of this act are each added to chapter 18.185 RCW.
