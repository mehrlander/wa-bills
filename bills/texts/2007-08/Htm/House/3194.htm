     AN ACT Relating to a master hunter program; and adding a new section to chapter 77.32 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 77.32 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The legislature intends to establish a master hunter program to encourage and enhance hunter/landowner relations. The master hunter program's purpose is to assist the department in effectively managing wildlife in areas and at times when both a high ethical standard and a demonstrated skill level are needed for resource protection and public safety. Individuals certified by the department to participate in the master hunter program may assist the department and private landowners with issues such as wildlife conservation, habitat protection, and wildlife damage.
     (2) The commission shall adopt rules for the administration of the master hunter program in order to avoid potential fraud and abuse. No person may qualify for the master hunter program under this section without first undergoing a criminal background check.
     (3) Persons who meet and maintain the master hunter program requirements may participate in special hunts or animal removals under chapters 77.12 and 77.36 RCW.
