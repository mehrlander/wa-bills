     AN ACT Relating to establishing a military family assistance program; adding new sections to chapter 43.330 RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   Members of the Washington national guard are currently serving to protect the security and well-being of the United States. However, as they are carrying out these duties, many of their families are left without the adequate means to provide for their security and well-being.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 43.330 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) There is established in the department a military family assistance program. The legislature intends that, to the extent of available funds, the program shall be available to provide temporary financial assistance to families with members currently serving in the Washington national guard whose gross family income at time of application does not exceed one hundred fifty percent of the federal poverty level as adjusted for household size and determined annually by the federal department of health and human services.
     (2) The department shall grant assistance to as many applicants as will utilize available funds less appropriate administrative costs of the department, not to exceed five percent of moneys appropriated to the military assistance program.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 43.330 RCW to read as follows:
     The department may receive such gifts, grants, and endowments from public or private sources as may be made from time to time, in trust or otherwise, for the use and benefit of the purposes of this chapter and expend the same or any income therefrom according to the terms of the gifts, grants, or endowments.
