     AN ACT Relating to repealing the state certificate of need process; creating a new section; repealing RCW 43.370.040, 70.38.015, 70.38.018, 70.38.025, 70.38.095, 70.38.105, 70.38.111, 70.38.115, 70.38.118, 70.38.125, 70.38.128, 70.38.135, 70.38.155, 70.38.156, 70.38.157, 70.38.158, 70.38.220, 70.38.230, 70.38.250, 70.38.905, 70.38.910, 70.38.911, 70.38.914, 70.38.915, 70.38.916, 70.38.918, and 70.38.920; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The certificate of need process established in chapter 70.38 RCW is hereby abolished. By November 15, 2009, the code reviser shall recommend to the legislature appropriate legislation that effectuates the purposes of this act by removing references to the certificate of need process in sections of the Revised Code of Washington not repealed by this act.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 43.370.040 (Department of health -- Certificate of need review program) and 2007 c 259 s 53;
