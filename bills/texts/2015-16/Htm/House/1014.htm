AN ACT Relating to antifreeze products; and amending RCW 19.94.544.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
Sec. 1.  RCW 19.94.544 and 2008 c 68 s 3 are each amended to read as follows:
The requirements of this section and RCW 19.94.540 and 19.94.542 shall not apply to the sale of a motor vehicle that contains engine coolant or antifreeze ((or to wholesale containers of fifty-five gallons or more of engine coolant or antifreeze)).
