     AN ACT Relating to infrastructure funding; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature recognizes the need to provide local governments with a new, stable, and broad source of funding for capital infrastructure. The legislature recognizes also the need to adequately fund growth in school districts and the need to provide relief to the ongoing affordable housing crisis.
