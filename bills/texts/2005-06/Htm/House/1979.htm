     AN ACT Relating to health departments; and adding a new section to chapter 70.08 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 70.08 RCW to read as follows:
     A local board of health operating under this chapter must include the mayor of the participating city and the county executive of the participating county. The mayor and the county executive shall alternately serve as chair of the local board of health.
