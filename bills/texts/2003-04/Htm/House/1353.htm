     AN ACT Relating to state route number 513; and amending RCW 47.17.695.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 47.17.695 and 1991 c 342 s 38 are each amended to read as follows:
     A state highway to be known as state route number 513 is established as follows:
     Beginning at a junction with state route number 520 in Seattle, ((thence)) then northerly ((and easterly)) to the vicinity of ((Sand Point)) Northeast Pacific Street.
