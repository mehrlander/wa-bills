     AN ACT Relating to naming the state bird; and amending RCW 1.20.040.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 1.20.040 and 1951 c 249 s 1 are each amended to read as follows:
     The ((willow goldfinch)) great blue heron is hereby designated as the official bird of the state of Washington.
