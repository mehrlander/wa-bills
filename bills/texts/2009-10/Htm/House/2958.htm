     AN ACT Relating to transfer or disposal of certain park lands; and adding a new section to chapter 79A.05 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 79A.05 RCW to read as follows:
     The commission shall adopt by rule criteria for the transfer, sale, or other disposal of land under RCW 79A.05.170 and 79A.05.175. The commission shall provide Indian tribes the right of first refusal to acquire lands that have archaeological, historical, and cultural significance to federally recognized Indian tribes whose traditional lands and territories included parts of Washington.
