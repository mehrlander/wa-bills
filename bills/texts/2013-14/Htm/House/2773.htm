     AN ACT Relating to the operation of wheeled all-terrain vehicles in recreation areas for persons with disabilities; amending RCW 46.19.030 and 46.63.020; adding a new section to chapter 46.09 RCW; prescribing penalties; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 46.09 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The department must issue, and design, an Americans with disabilities act parking sticker for placement on a wheeled all-terrain vehicle, authorizing access to recreation areas that are difficult to access by persons with disabilities, to an applicant that meets the following requirements:
     (a) The applicant's disability must be determined by a person listed under RCW 46.19.010(2) and recorded on a certification document designed and administered by the department, establishing that the applicant meets one of the following criteria:
     (i) Loss of one or both legs or feet;
     (ii) Inability to walk more than two hundred feet without having to stop and rest;
     (iii) Inability to walk due to severe disability where a person cannot walk without the use of or assistance from a brace, crutches, walker, cane, prosthetic device, wheel chair, other assistive device, or another person;
     (iv) Lung disease from which the person's expiratory volume for one second is less than one liter when measured by spirometry or from which the person's arterial oxygen is less than 60 mm/hg of room air at rest;
     (v) Severely limited in ability to walk due to arthritic, neurological, or orthopedic condition;
     (vi) Uses portable oxygen;
     (vii) Cardiovascular disease from which the person's functional limitations are classified as class III or IV under standards adopted by the American heart association;
     (viii) Cardiovascular disease from which a marked limitation of physical activity causes fatigue, palpitation, dyspnea, or anginal pain; or
     (ix) Other disease or disorder including, but not limited to, severe arthritis or neurological orthopedic impairment that creates a severe mobility limitation; and
     (b) The wheeled all-terrain vehicle meets all registration requirements under this chapter.
     (2)(a) The Americans with disabilities act parking sticker must be placed on the rear of the wheeled all-terrain vehicle.
     (b) The Americans with disabilities act parking sticker must be renewed every three years at the time of annual registration renewal for the wheeled all-terrain vehicle. If the person's disability is temporary, the sticker expires on the date submitted for recovery by the physician as listed on the certification document.
     (c) The department may by rule establish a fee to cover the cost of designing, producing, and administering the Americans with disabilities act parking sticker.
     (3) A person must carry either a Washington state driver's license or identicard when operating a wheeled all-terrain vehicle with an Americans with disabilities act parking sticker.
     (4) The privileges granted under this section may extend to one companion of the operator of a wheeled all-terrain vehicle with an Americans with disabilities act parking sticker serving as operator or passenger of the vehicle if the vehicle is designed for passenger use.
     (5) The submittal of fraudulent physician certification documents submitted to the department to obtain an Americans with disabilities act parking sticker is a gross misdemeanor.
     (6) The department may adopt rules to implement and administer this section.

Sec. 2   RCW 46.19.030 and 2010 c 161 s 704 are each amended to read as follows:
     (1) The department shall design special license plates for persons with disabilities, parking placards, and year tabs displaying the international symbol of access.
     (2) Special license plates for persons with disabilities must be displayed on the motor vehicle as standard issue license plates as described in RCW 46.16A.200.
     (3) Parking placards must be displayed when the motor vehicle is parked by suspending it from the rearview mirror. In the absence of a rearview mirror, the parking placard must be displayed on the dashboard.
     (4) Special year tabs for persons with disabilities must be displayed on license plates as defined by the department.
     (5)(a) Persons who have been issued special license plates for persons with disabilities, parking placards, or special license plates with a special year tab for persons with disabilities may park in places reserved for persons with physical disabilities.
     (b) Persons who possess an Americans with disabilities act parking sticker as issued under section 1 of this act on his or her wheeled all-terrain vehicle may park the wheeled all-terrain vehicle in places reserved for persons with physical disabilities.
