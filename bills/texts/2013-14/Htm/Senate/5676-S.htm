     AN ACT Relating to protecting personal voter signatures; and adding a new section to chapter 42.56 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 42.56 RCW to read as follows:
     The signature of a voter who signs a petition that has been submitted to an agency is exempt from disclosure under this chapter.
