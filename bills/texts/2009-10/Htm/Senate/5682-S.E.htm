     AN ACT Relating to the realignment of transportation regions; and adding a new section to chapter 47.01 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 47.01 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The legislature recognizes that the urban corridors office within the department operates as a seventh administrative region. Therefore, the legislature intends that the regional consolidation in this section will result in a decrease in the number of total regions. This consolidation will also result in a reduction in the amount of regional management level staff by at least five percent by July 1, 2011.
     (2) Beginning in January 2010 and completed by July 1, 2011, as a means of reducing department administrative costs and providing more efficient use of department resources, the secretary shall realign the transportation regions to generally include:
     (a) The Olympic region, containing Clallam, Jefferson, Grays Harbor, Mason, Lewis, Kitsap, and Thurston counties;
     (b) The southwest region, containing Pacific, Wahkiakum, Cowlitz, Clark, and Skamania counties;
     (c) The northwest region, containing San Juan, Island, Whatcom, and Skagit counties;
     (d) The Puget Sound region, containing King, Pierce, and Snohomish counties;
     (e) The south central region, containing Chelan, Douglas, Grant, Kittitas, Yakima, Benton, Franklin, and Klickitat counties, with a connection into Walla Walla county along state route number 12 to the Tri-Cities area; and
     (f) The eastern region, containing Okanogan, Ferry, Stevens, Pend Oreille, Lincoln, Spokane, Adams, Whitman, Columbia, Garfield, and Asotin counties, and that portion of Walla Walla county not included in (e) of this subsection.
