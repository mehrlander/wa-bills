     AN ACT Relating to fossil fuel production; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature intends to establish a methodology of collecting royalties for fossil fuel production in the state of Washington. Further, the legislature intends to establish a system that is consistent with other systems used in the Pacific Northwest.
