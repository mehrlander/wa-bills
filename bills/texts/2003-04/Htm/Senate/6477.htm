     AN ACT Relating to imposing impact fees on manufactured housing communities; and adding a new section to chapter 82.02 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.02 RCW to read as follows:
     Manufactured housing communities, as defined in RCW 59.20.030, are attached, multifamily housing units for the purpose of imposing impact fees under this chapter.
