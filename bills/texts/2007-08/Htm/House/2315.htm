     The economic table is presumptive for combined monthly net incomes up to and including ((five)) six thousand dollars. When combined monthly net income exceeds ((five)) six thousand dollars, support shall not be set at an amount lower than the presumptive amount of support set for combined monthly net incomes of ((five)) six thousand dollars unless the court finds a reason to deviate below that amount. The economic table is advisory but not presumptive for combined monthly net incomes that exceed ((five)) six thousand dollars. When combined monthly net income exceeds ((seven)) eight thousand four hundred dollars, the court may set support at an advisory amount of support set for combined monthly net incomes between ((five)) six thousand and ((seven)) eight thousand four hundred dollars or the court may exceed the advisory amount of support set for combined monthly net incomes of ((seven)) eight thousand four hundred dollars upon written findings of fact.
