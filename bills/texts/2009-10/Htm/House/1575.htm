     AN ACT Relating to a state route number 2 route development plan; creating a new section; making an appropriation; and providing an expiration date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The department of transportation shall conduct a state route number 2 route development plan that will identify essential improvements needed between the port of Everett/Naval Station and approaching the state route number 9 interchange near the city of Snohomish.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   The sum of four hundred thousand dollars, or as much thereof as may be necessary, is appropriated for the biennium ending June 30, 2011, from the multimodal transportation account to the department of transportation for the purposes of section 1 of this act.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act expires July 1, 2011.
