     AN ACT Relating to rainwater collection facilities; and adding a new section to chapter 90.03 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 90.03 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) In order to qualify for the exemptions to rainwater collection facilities, the owner of a rain barrel, cistern, or other similar water storage facility that has a storage capacity of greater than three hundred gallons must intend to put the stored water to beneficial use on the property where the rainwater was collected and manage the water stored in the facility in compliance with rules developed by the department under this section.
     (2) The department shall adopt rules to specify conditions under which rainwater collection facilities may be constructed and operated in order to protect existing water rights and water quality and to prevent the reduction of stream flows to a level detrimental to instream values. At a minimum, the rules must address consideration of annual rainfall, population density, and discharge impact from rainwater collection facilities. In developing these rules, the department shall work with the representatives of a broad group of interested parties, individuals with technical expertise, and project hosts to adopt rules under this section for permitting rainwater collection facilities.
     (3) Rainwater collected under this exemption does not result in a water right under RCW 90.03.250 or 90.03.370.
     (4)(a) Any person owning or using a rain barrel, cistern, or other similar water storage facility greater than three hundred gallons that was installed before the effective date of this section may continue to use the facility without obtaining a permit under RCW 90.03.250 or 90.03.370 until the department adopts a final rule governing the use of rain barrels, cisterns, or other similar water storage facilities. After the adoption of final rules, all persons using rain barrels, cisterns, or other similar water storage facilities must comply with the final rules.
     (b) A permit issued under RCW 90.03.250 or 90.03.370 is not required in circumstances where the use of rainwater collection and use systems is located on an island where groundwater and surface water availability is limited due to saltwater intrusion.
     (5) The department shall report to the appropriate committees of the legislature by December 31, 2008, regarding the implementation of this section. After June 1, 2009, the department shall proceed with permanent rule making to establish exemptions by rule for rainwater systems consistent with this section unless the legislature acts to direct otherwise.
