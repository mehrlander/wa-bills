     AN ACT Relating to permitting certain higher education employees to engage in collective bargaining; and adding a new section to chapter 41.56 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 41.56 RCW to read as follows:
     In addition to the entities listed in RCW 41.56.020, this chapter applies to full-time and part-time University of Washington extension lecturers in English language programs holding annual and quarterly contracts.
