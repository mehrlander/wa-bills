     AN ACT Relating to prohibiting public agencies from providing specific segments of the general public with access to land for the purposes of hunting and fishing; adding a new section to chapter 77.12 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 43.17 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 79A.05 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 79.10 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 36.32 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 35.21 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 35A.21 RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 77.12 RCW to read as follows:
     Unless otherwise specifically authorized in statute, no public agency, as that term is defined in RCW 42.30.020, may provide only a specified segment or segments of the general public with access to land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the public agency for the purpose of hunting or fishing without providing the same access opportunity to the general public. This section applies to rules or regulations of the public agency, access contracts and agreements, and all other forms of express or implied access permission.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   Any public agency, as that term is defined in RCW 42.30.020, that allows a specified segment or segments of the general public to access land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the public agency for the purpose of hunting or fishing as of the effective date of this section must either suspend access permission to the specified segment or segments or allow access to the general public.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 43.17 RCW to read as follows:
     Any department listed in RCW 43.17.010 shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the department.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   A new section is added to chapter 79A.05 RCW to read as follows:
     The commission shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the commission for hunting or fishing access.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 5   A new section is added to chapter 79.10 RCW under the subchapter heading "Multiple Use" to read as follows:
     The department shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the department.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 6   A new section is added to chapter 36.32 RCW to read as follows:
     A county shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the county.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 7   A new section is added to chapter 35.21 RCW to read as follows:
     A city shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the city.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 8   A new section is added to chapter 35A.21 RCW to read as follows:
     A code city shall comply with section 1 of this act when granting access for hunting or fishing to any land owned, leased, or otherwise controlled by the code city.
