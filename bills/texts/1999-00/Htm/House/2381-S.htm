    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 30.  A new section is added to 1999 c 379 (uncodified) to read as follows:

    Salmon Recovery Program

 

    The appropriation in this section is provided to the following agencies for the following purposes:

 

    (1) For the Department of Fish and Wildlife

    (a) $100,000 is provided solely to implement methods to reduce the by-catch of listed salmon stocks.

    (b) $2,000,000 is provided solely for the following purposes in the Methow river valley:

    (i) $500,000 to develop baseline hydrological data for the Methow river;

    (ii) $750,000 to purchase water rights pursuant to chapter ... (Senate Bill No. 6525), Laws of 2000.  If chapter ... (Senate Bill No. 6525), Laws of 2000 is not enacted by June 30, 2000, this subsection (1)(b)(ii) is null and void and $250,000 of the appropriation in this subsection (1)(b)(ii) shall be used for the purposes of (b)(iii) of this subsection and $500,000 shall be used for the same purpose as section 18(3) of this act; and

    (iii) $750,000 to provide salmonid screening.  Any such screening provided for a point of diversion for a water right in the Methow river valley shall be sized to accommodate:  (A) Not less than the annual amount of water diverted at that point of diversion during any of the five years beginning with calendar year 1994 and ending with calendar year 1998; and (B) not less than the instantaneous amount of water diverted at that point of diversion at the peak of diversion during any of those five calendar years.

    (c) $950,000 is provided solely for grants to lead entities established in accordance with RCW 75.46.060.

    (d) $880,000 is provided solely for contracted engineering services to lead entities or community-based organizations to implement habitat projects.

    (e) $5,000,000 is provided to buy back commercial licenses for salmon fishers.  If the department of fish and wildlife is allocated $14,000,000 or more of federal money during the biennium to buy back commercial fish licenses, the appropriation in this subsection (1)(e) shall be distributed sixty percent to buy back Puget Sound purse seine, gill net, and reef net commercial salmon licenses and forty percent to other commercial salmon licenses.  If the department is allocated less than $14,000,000 of federal money during the biennium to buy back commercial fish licenses, the appropriation in this subsection (1)(e) shall be distributed eighty percent to buy back Puget Sound purse seine, gill net, and reef net commercial salmon licenses and twenty percent to other commercial salmon licenses.

 

    (2) For the State Conservation Commission

    $1,000,000 is provided solely for grants to the Columbia ground water management area for the irrigation water management cost share program and for public health information to households with at-risk infant water users.

 

    (3) For the Department of Agriculture

    $150,000 is provided solely for the agriculture, fish, and water negotiation process. If an appropriation equal to or more than the appropriation in this subsection (3) for the same purpose is provided in the omnibus operations appropriations act, the appropriation in this subsection (3) shall be used for the same purpose as section 18(3) of this act.

 

    (4) For the Interagency Committee for Outdoor Recreation

    $250,000 is provided solely for the development of a strategic plan to improve the accuracy and availability of information needed for planning and managing natural resources in the state.  The plan shall address the collection, analysis, and management of information consistent with the national spacial data infrastructure framework being supported by the Washington geographic information council.

    (5) $2,000,000 is provided solely for deposit into the advanced environmental mitigation revolving account created in RCW 47.12.340 to mitigate the adverse impacts upon the environment from transportation projects.  The amounts deposited into the account shall be used for salmonid habitat mitigation.
