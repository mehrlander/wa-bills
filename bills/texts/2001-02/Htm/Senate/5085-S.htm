_1      AN ACT Relating to administering emergency services to school

_2  students experiencing severe hypoglycemia; adding new sections to

_3  chapter 28A.210 RCW; and adding a new section to chapter 18.79

_4  RCW.

     

_5  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_6      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  A new section is added to chapter 28A.210

_7  RCW to read as follows:

_8      Public school districts may provide for the administration of

_9  glucagon to students who experience severe hypoglycemia when the

10  students are in the custody of the school district, pursuant to

11  chapter 18.79 RCW, if the glucagon is provided in compliance with:

12      (1) Rules adopted under section 3 of this act by the state

13  nursing care quality assurance commission, and the instructions of

14  a registered nurse or advanced registered nurse practitioner

15  issued under the rules; and

16      (2) Written policies of the school district that are adopted to

17  implement this section and are developed in accordance with

18  chapters 41.56 and 41.59 RCW.

_1      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  A new section is added to chapter 28A.210

_2  RCW to read as follows:

_3      (1) If a school employee provides for the administration of

_4  glucagon to a student under chapter 18.79 RCW and this chapter in

_5  substantial compliance with:  (a) Rules adopted by the state nursing

_6  care quality assurance commission and the instructions of a

_7  registered nurse or advanced registered nurse practitioner issued

_8  under such rules, and (b) written policies of the school district,

_9  then the employee, the employee's school district and school of

10  employment shall not be liable in any criminal action or for civil

11  damages in their individual, marital, governmental, corporate, or

12  other capacity as a result of providing glucagon.

13      (2) Administering glucagon to any student under chapter 18.79

14  RCW and this chapter may be discontinued by a public school

15  district, and the school district, school, or its employees shall

16  not be liable in any criminal action or for civil damages in their

17  individual, marital, governmental, corporate, or other capacity as

18  a result of the discontinuance:  PROVIDED, That the public school

19  district has first provided actual notice orally or in writing in

20  advance of the date of discontinuance to a parent or legal

21  guardian of the student or other person having legal control over

22  the student:  PROVIDED FURTHER, That the public school district

23  otherwise provides for the administration of glucagon of the

24  student to the extent required by federal or state law.

25      (3) To be eligible to administer glucagon, an employee not

26  licensed under chapter 18.79 RCW, shall file, without coercion by

27  the employer, a voluntary written, current, and unexpired letter

28  of intent stating the employee's willingness to administer

29  glucagon in compliance with established guidelines.  If a public

30  school employee who is not licensed under chapter 18.79 RCW

31  chooses not to file a letter under this section, the employee

32  shall not be subject to any employer reprisal or disciplinary

33  action for refusing to file a letter.

     

34      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  A new section is added to chapter 18.79

35  RCW to read as follows:

36      (1) In accordance with the rules adopted by the commission,
