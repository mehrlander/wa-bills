     AN ACT Relating to state tax incentives to encourage the redevelopment of port district property; adding a new section to chapter 82.04 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.14 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.29A RCW; providing an effective date; and providing expiration dates.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.04 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) Persons subject to payment of the tax under this chapter may deduct any amounts received for the following activities occurring within a port district business redevelopment zone:
     (a) Making sales at retail or wholesale;
     (b) Manufacturing or processing for hire; or
     (c) Performing any service.
     (2) For the purpose of this section, "port district business redevelopment zone" has the same meaning as provided in section 3 of this act.
     (3) This section expires January 1, 2025.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 82.14 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) A city legislative authority may fix and impose a sales and use tax in accordance with the terms of this chapter for the purposes designated in subsection (3) of this section.
     (2) The tax authorized in this section must be deducted from the amount of tax otherwise required to be collected or paid over to the department under chapter 82.08 or 82.12 RCW. The rate of tax may not exceed one-tenth of one percent of the selling price in the case of sales tax, or value of the article used, in the case of a use tax.
     (3) Moneys received from any tax imposed under this section must be used solely for the purpose of public improvement costs within a port district business redevelopment zone.
     (4) The definitions in this subsection apply throughout this section.
     (a) "Port district business redevelopment zone" has the same meaning as provided in section 3 of this act.
     (b) "Public improvement costs" has the same meaning as provided in RCW 39.102.020.
     (5) This section expires January 1, 2025.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 82.29A RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The leasehold excise tax under RCW 82.29A.030 does not apply to leasehold interests in real or personal property located within a port district business redevelopment zone.
     (2) The exemption provided in subsection (1) of this section does not apply to city and county leasehold excise taxes imposed under RCW 82.29A.040.
     (3) For the purposes of this section, "port district business redevelopment zone" means any area:
     (a) Acquired by a port district with a population of one million five hundred thousand or more for the purpose of airport expansion or aircraft noise mitigation, and the area is currently designated by a city for redevelopment for business or commercial uses;
     (b) Any area owned by the state of Washington as of January 1, 2010, and located adjacent to an area described under (a) of this subsection; and
     (c) Any area within one thousand seven hundred fifty feet of the boundary of an area described in (a) or (b) of this subsection.
     (4) This section expires January 1, 2025.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   This act takes effect July 1, 2011.
