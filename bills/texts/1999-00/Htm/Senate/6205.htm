Defining employment terms.

    AN ACT Relating to definitions of employment relationships; and adding a new chapter to Title 49 RCW.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  LEGISLATIVE INTENT.  Recent efforts to study the contingent work force in Washington state have focused, in part, on the definitions of terms used to describe certain employment relationships.  These efforts have revealed that federal and state laws contain distinct and sometimes conflicting definitions of these terms.  Likewise, government agencies and interested parties often use conflicting definitions of these terms.  The legislature finds that common definitions of these terms would aid these efforts, and ensure that their results could be more easily compared and contrasted.  For this reason, the legislature intends to adopt common definitions for the sole purpose of guiding efforts undertaken by the state, its institutions, and its agencies to study the contingent work force, and framing policy discussions related to the contingent work force.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  DEFINITIONS.  The definitions in this section shall be used solely to guide efforts undertaken by the state, its institutions, and its agencies to study the contingent work force, and to frame policy discussions related to the contingent work force.  (1) "Employee" means any individual who performs services for wages, salary, or other reimbursement under any contract of hire, written or oral, express or implied, with an employer.

    (2) "Employer" means any person who employs one or more employees.

    (3) "Full-time employee" means any employee who works forty hours per workweek or the number of hours per workweek that the employer considers to be full-time, whichever is less.

    (4) "Independent contractor" means any independent contractor as defined by common law.

    (5) "Part-time employee" means any employee who is not full-time.

    (6) "Permanent employee" means any employee who is hired for an indefinite period of time.

    (7) "Permatemp" means any permanent employee who performs the same work as or work comparable to that performed by other permanent employees, but who the employer classifies as a temporary employee.

    (8) "Temporary agency" means any person regularly undertaking with or without compensation to procure employees for an employer temporarily or to procure for employees opportunities to work for an employer.

    (9) "Temporary employee" means any employee who is not permanent.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  Captions used in this act are not any part of the law.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4.  Sections 1 through 3 of this act constitute a new chapter in Title 49 RCW.
