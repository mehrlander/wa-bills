     AN ACT Relating to restricting sex offenders' access to schools; amending RCW 9A.44.190; reenacting and amending RCW 9.94A.515; adding a new section to chapter 9A.44 RCW; prescribing penalties; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 9A.44 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) A person is guilty of criminal trespass on school property by a sex offender if he or she is a covered offender and knowingly enters the premises of a school.
     (2) This section does not apply if the person:
     (a) Is eighteen years of age and is a student attending or planning to attend the school; or
     (b) Is the parent or legal guardian of a child who attends the school and has written permission from the school principal or administrator to enter school premises for a specified purpose, including, but not limited to, attending a parent-teacher conference or participating in other educational or recreational activities.
     (3)(a) Except as provided in (b) of this subsection, criminal trespass on school property by a sex offender is a class C felony.
     (b) Criminal trespass on school property by a sex offender is a class B felony if the person has previously been convicted under this section.

Sec. 2   RCW 9A.44.190 and 2006 c 126 s 4 are each amended to read as follows:
     As used in this section and RCW 9A.44.193 ((and)), 9A.44.196, and section 1 of this act:
     (1) "Covered entity" means any public facility or private facility whose primary purpose, at any time, is to provide for the education, care, or recreation of a child or children, including but not limited to community and recreational centers, playgrounds, schools, swimming pools, and state or municipal parks.
     (2) "Child" means a person under the age of eighteen, unless the context clearly indicates that the term is otherwise defined in statute.
     (3) "Public facility" means a facility operated by a unit of local or state government, or by a nonprofit organization.
     (4) "Schools" means public and private schools, but does not include home-based instruction as defined in RCW 28A.225.010.
     (5) "Covered offender" means a person required to register under RCW 9A.44.130 who is eighteen years of age or older, who is not under the jurisdiction of the juvenile rehabilitation authority or currently serving a special sex offender disposition alternative, whose risk level classification has been assessed at a risk level II or a risk level III pursuant to RCW 72.09.345, and who, at any time, has been convicted of one or more of the following offenses:
     (a) Rape of a child in the first, second, and third degree; child molestation in the first, second, and third degree; indecent liberties against a child under age fifteen; sexual misconduct with a minor in the first and second degree; incest in the first and second degree; luring with sexual motivation; possession of depictions of minors engaged in sexually explicit conduct; dealing in depictions of minors engaged in sexually explicit conduct; bringing into the state depictions of minors engaged in sexually explicit conduct; sexual exploitation of a minor; communicating with a minor for immoral purposes; ((patronizing a juvenile prostitute)) commercial sexual abuse of a minor;
     (b) Any felony in effect at any time prior to March 20, 2006, that is comparable to an offense listed in (a) of this subsection, including, but not limited to, statutory rape in the first and second ((degrees [degree])) degree and carnal knowledge;
     (c) Any felony offense for which:
     (i) There was a finding that the offense was committed with sexual motivation; and
     (ii) The victim of the offense was less than sixteen years of age at the time of the offense;
     (d) An attempt, conspiracy, or solicitation to commit any of the offenses listed in (a) through (c) of this subsection;
     (e) Any conviction from any other jurisdiction which is comparable to any of the offenses listed in (a) through (d) of this subsection.
