     AN ACT Relating to providing a business and occupation tax deduction for amounts physicians receive from medicaid or medicare when the amount is less than the cost of certain prescription drugs; and adding a new section to chapter 82.04 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.04 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) In computing tax there may be deducted from the measure of tax amounts received by physicians licensed under chapter 18.71 RCW for physician-administered oncology prescription drugs, when the amount received is less than the cost of the prescription drug covered under: (a) A medical assistance program established under RCW 74.09.500 or (b) a federal medicare program authorized under Title XVIII of the federal social security act.
     (2) The deduction authorized by this section does not apply to amounts received from patient copayments or patient deductibles.
     (3) The physician must keep records necessary for the department to verify eligibility under this section.
