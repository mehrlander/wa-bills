     AN ACT Relating to a finding of necessity for administrative rules; and adding new sections to chapter 34.05 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 34.05 RCW to read as follows:
     Before proposing a new rule or amending an existing rule that meets the definition of a significant legislative rule under RCW 34.05.328, a state agency shall adopt a finding of necessity demonstrating that the new or amended rule is necessary to implement a new legislative or federal directive, or is required to fully implement the intent of an existing state or federal law. The process includes the following steps:
     (1) The state agency shall identify the legislation or federal law that enables or directs the agency to adopt or to amend the applicable rule.
     (2) The state agency shall analyze and clearly define how and why the proposed new or amended rule is required to achieve the intent of the enabling legislation. The state agency shall propose conclusions of law stating the authority of the agency to adopt or amend the applicable rule.
     (3) The state agency shall propose findings of fact based upon an analysis of the best available science or best available information. The findings must support a conclusion that the new or amended rule requirements are supported by science or reputable information.
     (4) The state agency shall propose findings of fact that demonstrate that the proposed new or amended rule is the least burdensome and most cost-effective alternative to effectuate the enabling legislation or federal law. The agency shall provide a cost-benefit analysis report.
     (5) The state agency shall file a notice of intent to adopt a finding of necessity with the code reviser for publication in the state register, establish a public comment period of thirty to sixty days after publication, and conduct at least four public hearings around the state, equally divided as to east and west, to take public testimony regarding the proposed conclusions of law, findings of fact, the agency director's proposed agency action, and the adoption of the finding of necessity.
     (6) Adoption of a finding of necessity and supporting conclusions of law and findings of fact are required before filing the notice of intent to adopt or amend a rule with the office of the code reviser under RCW 34.05.320.
     (7) The action of adopting a finding of necessity may be appealed to the superior court of Thurston county or of the county of residence of the appellant.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 34.05 RCW to read as follows:
     Agencies shall file proposed findings of necessity under section 1 of this act by June 1st of each year. The final findings of necessity must be filed by September 15th of each year.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 34.05 RCW to read as follows:
     A finding by a superior court that the state agency failed to comply with the process for adoption of a finding of necessity under section 1 of this act renders a finding of necessity and a significant legislative rule adopted under it null and void.
