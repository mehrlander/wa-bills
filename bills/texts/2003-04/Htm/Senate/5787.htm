     AN ACT Relating to the use of a leaching test in state water quality certifications; and adding a new section to chapter 90.48 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 90.48 RCW to read as follows:
     In order to ensure that major construction projects involving the use of large quantities of fill material do not pose a threat to water quality, the department shall require that the chemical suitability of potential fill material be determined through use of a leaching test of the type specified in WAC 173-340-747(7), as it existed on January 1, 2003, in all water quality certifications issued under section 401 of the federal clean water act and administrative orders issued under this chapter where such certification or administrative order authorizes the placement of more than two million cubic yards of fill material, some or all of which will be placed in waters of the state. This requirement must be retroactively applied to all water quality certifications and administrative orders issued by the department that authorized the placement of more than two million cubic yards of fill material where the placement is not substantially completed by June 1, 2003. This section does not apply to section 401 certifications and orders authorizing the placement of fill material that has been dredged from waters of the United States.
