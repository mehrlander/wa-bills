AN ACT Relating to special antlerless deer hunting seasons; and adding a new section to chapter 77.32 RCW.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. A new section is added to chapter 77.32 RCW to read as follows:
The department shall allow a person age sixty-five or over to harvest either an antlerless mule deer or antlerless white-tailed deer during the modern firearm general white-tailed buck season in all game management units east of the crest of the Cascade mountains.
