AN ACT Relating to spinal manipulation endorsement requirements for physical therapists; repealing 2014 c 116 s 2; repealing 2014 c 116 s 7 (uncodified); and providing an effective date.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
(1) 2014 c 116 s 2; and
(2) 2014 c 116 s 7 (uncodified).
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2. This act takes effect June 15, 2020.
