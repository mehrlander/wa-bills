AN ACT Relating to legal financial obligations; amending RCW 3.50.100, 3.62.020, 3.62.040, 3.66.120, 6.17.020, 9.92.060, 9.94A.725, 9.94A.750, 9.94A.753, 9.94A.760, 9.95.210, 10.01.160, 10.01.170, 10.01.185, 10.05.140, 10.05.170, 10.64.015, 10.64.120, 10.82.070, 10.101.020, 35.20.220, 36.18.016, 36.18.020, 4.56.110, 7.68.035, 9.92.070, 9.94A.6333, 9.94B.040, 10.01.090, 10.01.180, and 43.79.505; adding new sections to chapter 10.01 RCW; and repealing RCW 3.62.085, 10.46.190, 10.73.160, 10.82.090, and 70.48.390.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. A new section is added to chapter 10.01 RCW to read as follows:
(1) A previously imposed judgment against an offender for any costs, fees, and interest on restitution or other legal financial obligations eliminated or repealed by this act is not enforceable after the effective date of this section. The court shall not accept payments for such costs and fees or for any accrued interest on restitution or nonrestitution legal financial obligations after the effective date of this section. Any such debts shall be considered null and void and satisfied and paid in full. Nothing in this section shall require a clerk to take any action or alter any record except by the lawful order of a judge.
(2) Nothing in this section shall prevent a court from granting individual relief at any time in response to a motion. On motion of an offender, the court shall waive all uncollectible portions of the offender's legal financial obligations, including accrued interest on restitution and nonrestitution legal financial obligations. The presiding judge of a superior court or court of limited jurisdiction may authorize an administrative process to waive outstanding debt for any uncollectible legal financial obligation, other than restitution, including accrued interest on restitution and nonrestitution legal financial obligations, imposed against an adult.
(3) For the purposes of this section, the clerk of the superior court or court administrator of a court of limited jurisdiction may seek a judicial order to waive outstanding debt for costs and fees, other than restitution, and for accrued interest on restitution and nonrestitution legal financial obligations, in the same manner as the clerk is authorized to seek an extension of jurisdiction under RCW 6.17.020 for purposes of collection as allowed under RCW 36.18.190. Any motion filed by a clerk or court administrator under this section does not constitute the practice of law.
