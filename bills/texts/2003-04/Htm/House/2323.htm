     AN ACT Relating to sales and use tax exemptions for low-income persons; adding a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.08 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) An eligible person is entitled to an annual sales and use tax exemption of four hundred fifty dollars per year. This exemption is available only through the use of a sales tax exemption card provided under this section. A sales tax exemption card issued under this section may be used only for exemption of taxes imposed under RCW 82.08.020, and chapters 82.12 and 82.14 RCW. An unused portion of an annual exemption amount expires at the end of the year for which it is provided, and may not be refunded or carried forward to subsequent years.
     (2) The department of revenue shall establish a sales tax exemption card program for the purposes of this section, using debit cards, stored-value cards, or similar electronic media. Only cards, electronic media, and procedures approved by the department may be used for exemption of sales and use taxes under this section. The department shall prescribe forms and procedures for issuance and administration of exemption cards. The department may contract with vendors for issuance and administration of the cards. The department may charge a fee, or authorize a fee to be charged, from each eligible person for the cost of issuing a card, not to exceed ten dollars per year per card. The department shall reconcile the fiscal impact of exemptions under this section among state accounts and local governments in an appropriate manner.
     (3) As used in this section, "eligible person" means a resident of this state who has a combined disposable income, as defined in RCW 84.36.383, that is less than one hundred fifty percent of the federal poverty level as adjusted for household size and determined annually by the federal department of health and human services.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   This act takes effect July 1, 2004.
