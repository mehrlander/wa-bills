     AN ACT Relating to display of the United States flag; and amending RCW 1.20.015.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 1.20.015 and 1955 c 88 s 1 are each amended to read as follows:
     The flag of the United States and the flag of the state shall be prominently installed, displayed and maintained in schools, court rooms, ((and)) state buildings, and ferry vessels operated by the department of transportation.
