     AN ACT Relating to bail for persons in custody; and adding a new section to chapter 10.19 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 10.19 RCW to read as follows:
     A person who is held in custody and is alleged to have committed a crime against a person, when the crime is a felony, shall appear before a judicial officer for an individualized determination of pretrial release conditions prior to the person's release from custody if the offense is bailable.
