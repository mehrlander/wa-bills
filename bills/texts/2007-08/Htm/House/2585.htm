     AN ACT Relating to the business and occupation taxation of newspaper-labeled supplements; and amending RCW 82.04.214.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 82.04.214 and 1994 c 22 s 1 are each amended to read as follows:
     "Newspaper" means a publication issued regularly at stated intervals at least twice a month and printed on newsprint in tabloid or broadsheet format folded loosely together without stapling, glue, or any other binding of any kind, or means any newspaper-labeled supplement produced in any media.
