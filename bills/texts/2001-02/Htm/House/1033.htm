_1      AN ACT Relating to local air authorities; and amending RCW

_2  70.94.100 and 70.94.130.

     

_3  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_4      Sec. 1.  RCW 70.94.100 and 1991 c 199 s 704 are each amended to

_5  read as follows:

_6      (1) The governing body of each authority shall be known as the

_7  board of directors.

_8      (2) In the case of an authority comprised of one county the

_9  board shall be comprised of two appointees of the city selection

10  committee, at least one of whom shall represent the city having

11  the most population in the county, and two representatives to be

12  designated by the board of county commissioners.  In the case of an

13  authority comprised of two, three, four, or five counties, the

14  board shall be comprised of one appointee from each county, who

15  shall represent the city having the most population in such

16  county, to be designated by the mayor and city council of such

17  city, and one representative from each county to be designated by

18  the board of county commissioners of each county making up the

                               p. 1                       HB 1033

_1  authority.  In the case of an authority comprised of six or more

_2  counties, the board shall be comprised of one representative from

_3  each county to be designated by the board of county commissioners

_4  of each county making up the authority, and three appointees, one

_5  each from the three largest cities within the local authority's

_6  jurisdiction to be appointed by the mayor and city council of such

_7  city.  Any authority comprised of two counties or more shall also

_8  include, at the legislator's discretion, a legislator from the

_9  district in which the counties are located selected by the

10  legislators from that district.  In the event the counties

11  comprising the authority are located in different legislative

12  districts, a legislator from the district that includes the

13  largest city by population shall be appointed by the group of

14  legislators representing those districts.

15      (3) If the board of an authority otherwise would consist of an

16  even number, the members selected as above provided shall agree

17  upon and elect an additional member who shall be either a member

18  of the governing body of one of the towns, cities or counties

19  comprising the authority, or a private citizen residing in the

20  authority.

21      (4) The terms of office of board members shall be four years.

22      (5) Wherever a member of a board has a potential conflict of

23  interest in an action before the board, the member shall declare

24  to the board the nature of the potential conflict prior to

25  ((participating in the action review.  The board shall, if the

26  potential conflict of interest, in the judgment of a majority of

27  the board, may prevent the member from a fair and objective review

28  of the case, remove the member from participation in the action))

29  the board's review of the action, and shall refrain from voting on

30  the action before the board.

31      (6) A member of the board of directors may not work in any

32  capacity for an entity that is regulated by a local air authority

33  of which the director is a member.  If a member of a board of

34  directors is offered a position of employment with an entity that

35  is, or in the past two years has been, regulated by the local air

36  authority of which the director is a member, the member is

37  required to choose between continued service on the board or the

38  employment position.

_1     Sec. 2.  RCW 70.94.130 and 1998 c 342 s 1 are each amended to read

_2  as follows:

_3      The board shall exercise all powers of the authority except as

_4  otherwise provided.  The board shall conduct its first meeting

_5  within thirty days after all of its members have been appointed or

_6  designated as provided in RCW 70.94.100.  The board shall meet at

_7  least ten times per year.  All meetings shall be publicly announced

_8  prior to their occurrence.  All meetings shall be open to the

_9  public.  A majority of the board shall constitute a quorum for the

10  transaction of business and shall be necessary for any action

11  taken by the board.  The board shall elect from ((its members)) the

12  elected officials on the board a chair and such other officers as

13  may be necessary.  Any member of the board may designate a regular

14  alternate to serve on the board in his or her place with the same

15  authority as the member when he or she is unable to attend.  In no

16  event may a regular alternate serve as the permanent chair.  Each

17  member of the board, or his or her representative, shall receive

18  from the authority compensation consistent with such authority's

19  rates (but not to exceed one thousand dollars per year) for time

20  spent in the performance of duties under this chapter, plus the

21  actual and necessary expenses incurred by the member in such

22  performance. The board may appoint a control officer, and any

23  other personnel, and shall determine their salaries, and pay same,

24  together with any other proper indebtedness, from authority funds.
