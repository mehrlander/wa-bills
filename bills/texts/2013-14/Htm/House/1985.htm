     AN ACT Relating to exempting future state transportation projects from state and local sales and use tax; adding a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; and adding a new section to chapter 82.12 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.08 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) Any person, including the department of transportation and any private entity or entities, is exempt from taxes on the site preparation for state transportation projects, the construction of state transportation projects, the acquisition of any related machinery and equipment that becomes a part of state transportation projects, and the rental of equipment for use in state transportation projects. Application must be made to the department in a form and manner prescribed by the department. The application must contain information regarding estimated or actual costs, time schedules for completion and operation, and other information required by the department. The department must approve the application within sixty days if it meets the requirements of this section.
     (2) The department must issue a sales and use tax exemption certificate for state and local sales and use taxes due under this chapter and chapters 82.12 and 82.14 RCW on the project.
     (3) Applications and any other information received by the department under this section are not confidential and are subject to disclosure.
     (4) The exemption authorized under this section applies to:
     (a) Any project where construction is initiated on or after the effective date of this section and the project is funded with new transportation revenues adopted on or after the effective date of this section; and
     (b) Any project where construction is initiated on or after July 1, 2017, and the project is funded with revenues adopted before the effective date of this section.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 82.12 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) This chapter does not apply to the use of machinery and equipment that becomes a part of a state transportation project or the use of rental equipment for use in a state transportation project.
     (2) The application requirements and other conditions under section 1 of this act apply to this section.
