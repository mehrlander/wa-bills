     AN ACT Relating to using certain state sales tax proceeds to maintain lifeguards at state parks; adding a new section to chapter 82.32 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.32 RCW to read as follows:
     The parks lifeguard account is created in the state treasury. The state treasurer shall deposit proceeds received under RCW 82.08.020 from the furnishing of lodging, or the granting of any similar license to use real property, if the real property is under the care, charge, control, or supervision of the Washington state parks and recreation commission. Moneys in the account may be spent only after appropriation. Expenditures from the account may be used only by the commission to maintain lifeguards at state parks.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   This act takes effect August 1, 2007.
