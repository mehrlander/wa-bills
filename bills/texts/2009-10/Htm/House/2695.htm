     AN ACT Relating to an online insurance verification system; adding new sections to chapter 46.30 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     The definitions in this section apply throughout this subchapter unless the context clearly requires otherwise.
     (1) "Commercial automobile insurance coverage" means any insurance coverage provided to an insured, regardless of the number of vehicles or entities covered, under a commercial insurance coverage form and rated using either a commercial manual or rating rule and without regard to vehicle type and ownership.
     (2) "Commercial motor vehicle" means any motor vehicle registered in this state and insured under commercial automobile insurance coverage.
     (3) "Insurer" means an insurance company licensed or authorized to do business in this state.
     (4) "Online insurance verification system" or "system" means an online insurance verification system using web services.
     (5) "Personal motor vehicle" means a motor vehicle registered in this state and insured under personal automobile insurance coverage and not under commercial automobile insurance coverage.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     (1) The department shall establish, within two years of the effective date of this act, an online insurance verification system using web services for motor vehicles registered in this state. In establishing this system, the department shall:
     (a) Consult regularly with representatives of insurers in determining the objectives and details of the system;
     (b) Provide for a testing period of more than nine months prior to the establishment date required in subsection (1) of this section;
     (c) Publish a detailed user guide; and
     (d) Establish and maintain a contact person for insurers.
     (2) In establishing this system, the department may enter into contracts with vendors or contractors.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     The online insurance verification system must:
     (1) Be accessible by authorized personnel of the department for direct inquiry. Access to the courts, insurers, and law enforcement must be through authorized personnel of the department;
     (2) Be capable of verifying, on an on demand basis minus reasonable down time for system maintenance as agreed upon by the state and the insurer that is within reasonable experientially based timeframes, the liability insurance status of a vehicle with the insurer listed on any insurance identification card or in the department's records. This verification must be made available through the internet or other similar electronic systems and must be consistent with insurance industry specifications and standards;
     (3) Be able to make insurance verification inquiries to insurers using multiple data elements including, but not limited to, national association of insurance commissioners code, vehicle identification numbers, policy numbers, or other data elements agreed to by the department and an insurer;
     (4) Provide sufficient measures for data security; and
     (5) Utilize open and agreed upon data and data transmission standards and standard schema.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     (1) Each insurer shall:
     (a) Cooperate with the department in establishing and operating the online insurance verification system;
     (b) Maintain the data necessary to verify the existence of mandatory liability coverage provided to its customers pursuant to the required time period for the online insurance verification system;
     (c) Maintain internet or similar service, pursuant to the requirements established for the online insurance verification system, through which online insurance verification can occur;
     (d) Provide adequate security consistent with insurance industry standards; and
     (e) Provide commercial automobile insurance customers with an insurance identification card clearly reflecting that the vehicle is insured under a commercial motor vehicle policy.
     (2) Each insurer is immune from civil and administrative liability for good faith efforts to comply with this subchapter.
     (3) Nothing in this subchapter prohibits an insurer from using the services of a vendor to facilitate the establishment and operation of the online insurance verification system required under this subchapter.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 5   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     (1) The department shall:
     (a) Cooperate with insurers in establishing and operating the system;
     (b) Maintain the list of authorized requesting entities and individuals and incorporate that list as part of the system;
     (c) Conduct a pilot project to test the system prior to the establishment date required under section 2(1) of this act;
     (d) Establish and maintain the systems necessary to make verification requests to insurers using the data elements agreed to by the department and the insurers;
     (e) For all information transmitted or received, implement and maintain strict system and data security measures consistent with applicable standards;
     (f) Keep all appropriate state agencies informed on the implementation status, functionality, and service interruptions of the system;
     (g) Provide alternative methods, as determined by the department, of reporting for small insurers writing policies for no more than five hundred vehicles in the state;
     (h) Work with insurers on issues related to the system to ensure equitable resolution;
     (i) Maintain historical records of system data for eighteen months; and
     (j) Provide a method to separately track motor vehicles owned by a qualified self-insured.
     (2) Nothing in this subchapter prohibits the department from using the services of a vendor to facilitate the establishment and operation of an online insurance verification system required under this subchapter.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 6   A new section is added to chapter 46.30 RCW under the subchapter heading "online insurance verification system" to read as follows:
     The department shall report to the transportation committees of the legislature by July 1, 2014, regarding the operation of the online insurance verification system including, but not limited to, the costs of system implementation and the effectiveness of the program in reducing the number of uninsured drivers.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 7   This act takes effect July 1, 2010.
