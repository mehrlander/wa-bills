     AN ACT Relating to exempting wholesale sales of bulk raw milk from business and occupation tax; and amending RCW 82.04.332.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 82.04.332 and 1998 c 312 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:
     This chapter does not apply to amounts received from buying bulk raw milk, wheat, oats, dry peas, dry beans, lentils, triticale, canola, corn, rye, and barley, but not including any manufactured products thereof, and selling the same at wholesale.
