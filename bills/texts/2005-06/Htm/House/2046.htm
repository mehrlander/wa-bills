     AN ACT Relating to tuition waivers for dependents of national guard members ordered to active duty to serve overseas in a war or conflict; amending RCW 28B.15.910; and adding a new section to chapter 28B.15 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 28B.15 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The legislature finds that when members of the Washington national guard are called into overseas active duty, their absence poses a hardship on their families in many ways. Frequently their compensation is reduced from its normal levels, making financial obligations more difficult to meet. Therefore, the legislature intends to support national guard members by waiving their children's tuition charges while they are on overseas active duty.
     (2) Subject to the limitations of RCW 28B.15.910, the governing boards of the state universities, the regional universities, The Evergreen State College, and the community colleges may waive all or a portion of tuition and fees for students who:
     (a) Are dependent children of members of the Washington national guard who have been ordered into federal active military service for a period exceeding thirty days to serve in a war or conflict fought on foreign soil; and
     (b) Qualify as a resident student under RCW 28B.15.012, except RCW 28B.15.012(2)(g).
     (3) If a governing board exercises the authority under this section, the board shall also adopt policies that provide a full or partial refund of tuition already paid for a quarter or semester during which a student becomes eligible for the waiver under this section.

Sec. 2   RCW 28B.15.910 and 2004 c 275 s 51 are each amended to read as follows:

As used in this section, "gross authorized operating fees revenue" means the estimated gross operating fees revenue as estimated under RCW 82.33.020 or as revised by the office of financial management, before granting any waivers. This limitation applies to all tuition waiver programs established before or after July 1, 1992.
