Extending the sunset date of the linked deposit program.  


    AN ACT Relating to extending the sunset date of the linked deposit program; and amending RCW 43.131.381 and 43.131.382.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    Sec. 1.  RCW 43.131.381 and 1994 c 126 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:

    The linked deposit program shall be terminated on June 30, ((2000)) 2004, as provided in RCW 43.131.382.

 

    Sec. 2.  RCW 43.131.382 and 1994 c 126 s 3 are each amended to read as follows:

    The following acts or parts of acts, as now existing or hereafter amended, are each repealed, effective June 30, ((2001)) 2005:

    (1) RCW 43.86A.060 and 1993 c 512 s 30;

    (2) RCW 43.63A.690 and 1993 c 512 s 31; and

    (3) RCW 43.86A.070 and 1993 c 512 s 34.
