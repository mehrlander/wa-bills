     AN ACT Relating to repeal of obsolete or superseded laws; and repealing RCW 9.58.110, 9.58.120, 49.44.100, 67.14.020, 67.14.040, 67.14.050, 67.14.060, 67.14.070, 67.14.080, 67.14.090, 67.14.100, 67.14.110, 67.14.120, 70.54.060, and 70.54.065.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 9.58.110 (Slander of woman) and 1909 c 249 s 181;
     (2) RCW 9.58.120 (Testimony necessary to convict) and 1927 c 90 s 1 & 1909 c 249 s 182;
     (3) RCW 49.44.100 (Bringing in out-of-state persons to replace employees involved in labor dispute -- Penalty) and 2003 c 53 s 277 & 1961 c 180 s 1;
     (4) RCW 67.14.020 (Sale or other disposition of liquor -- County license -- Penalty) and 1873 p 437 s 2;
     (5) RCW 67.14.040 (Retail liquor license) and 1973 1st ex.s. c 154 s 100, 1875 p 124 s 1, & 1873 p 438 s 4;
     (6) RCW 67.14.050 (Wholesale liquor license -- Billiard table, bowling alley licenses) and 1873 p 438 s 5;
     (7) RCW 67.14.060 (Liquor sales, keeping games, without license--Penalty) and 1873 p 439 s 6;
     (8) RCW 67.14.070 (Purchase of license -- Bond) and 1873 p 439 s 7;
     (9) RCW 67.14.080 (Duration of license) and 1873 p 439 s 8;
     (10) RCW 67.14.090 (Issuance of license) and 1873 p 439 s 9;
     (11) RCW 67.14.100 (When contrivance deemed kept for hire) and 1873 p 440 s 10;
     (12) RCW 67.14.110 (Druggists excepted) and 1873 p 440 s 11;
     (13) RCW 67.14.120 (Disposition of fees, fines, and forfeitures) and 1987 c 202 s 226, 1969 ex.s. c 199 s 29, & 1873 p 440 s 12;
     (14) RCW 70.54.060 (Ambulances and drivers) and 1945 c 65 s 1; and
     (15) RCW 70.54.065 (Ambulances and drivers -- Penalty) and 1945 c 65 s 2.
