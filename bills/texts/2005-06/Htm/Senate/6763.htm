     AN ACT Relating to encouraging the use of hydroelectric power as a renewable energy resource; adding a new section to chapter 43.21F RCW; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that:
     (1) Water is a clean and sustainable resource that produces clean, efficient, and low-cost power;
     (2) There is an increasing demand for clean and renewable power;
     (3) Hydroelectric power is an abundant and reliable source of power with potential for growth;
     (4) Hydroelectric power makes a valuable contribution to the state's electricity network and accounts for more than half of the state's overall fuel mix; and
     (5) Hydroelectric power balances the needs of the environment with the need for clean, nonpolluting, reliable, and abundant electricity for the electrical market.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 43.21F RCW to read as follows:
     It is the policy of the state of Washington to recognize hydroelectric power as a renewable energy resource and to encourage its use.
