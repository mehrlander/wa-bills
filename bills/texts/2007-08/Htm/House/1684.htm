     AN ACT Relating to effective and expiration dates for the clean fuel sales and use tax exemptions; amending 2005 c 296 s 5 (uncodified); and amending 2005 c 296 s 6 (uncodified).

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   2005 c 296 s 5 (uncodified) is amended to read as follows:
     This act takes effect ((January 1, 2009)) August 1, 2007.

Sec. 2   2005 c 296 s 6 (uncodified) is amended to read as follows:
     This act expires ((January 1, 2011)) June 30, 2017.
