     AN ACT Relating to body gripping traps; repealing RCW 77.15.192, 77.15.194, 77.15.196, and 77.15.198; repealing 2001 c 1 s 1 (uncodified); and repealing 2001 c 1 s 6 (uncodified).

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 77.15.192 (Definitions) and 2001 c 1 s 2;
     (2) RCW 77.15.194 (Unlawful traps) and 2001 c 1 s 3;
     (3) RCW 77.15.196 (Unlawful poison) and 2001 c 1 s 4;
     (4) RCW 77.15.198 (Violation of RCW 77.15.194 or 77.15.196--Penalty) and 2001 c 1 s 5;
     (5) 2001 c 1 s 1 (uncodified); and
     (6) 2001 c 1 s 6 (uncodified).
