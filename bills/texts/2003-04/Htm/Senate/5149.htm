     AN ACT Relating to preventing businesses from taking multiple tax credits on the same employment positions; and amending RCW 82.62.090.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 82.62.090 and 2000 c 106 s 9 are each amended to read as follows:
     (((1))) A person is not eligible to receive a credit under this chapter if the person is receiving credit for the same position under RCW 82.04.44525 or 82.04.4456.
     (((2) This section expires December 31, 2003.))
