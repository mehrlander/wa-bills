     AN ACT Relating to the eligibility of foreign medical school graduates for licensing as physician assistants; and amending RCW 18.71A.045.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 18.71A.045 and 1994 sp.s. c 9 s 322 are each amended to read as follows:
     ((Foreign medical school graduates shall not be eligible for licensing as physician assistants after July 1, 1989.)) Graduates of foreign medical schools shall comply with the licensing requirements set out in this chapter and by the commission in order to be licensed as physician assistants.
