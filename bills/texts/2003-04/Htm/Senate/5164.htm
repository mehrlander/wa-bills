     AN ACT Relating to oyster harvesting; and adding a new section to chapter 77.32 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 77.32 RCW to read as follows:
     A person of disability, age sixty-five or older, may take the daily limit of oysters off the beach without shucking the oysters.
