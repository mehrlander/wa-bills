AN ACT Relating to jobs training for homeless individuals; and adding a new section to chapter 43.185C RCW.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. A new section is added to chapter 43.185C RCW to read as follows:
Subject to the availability of amounts appropriated for this specific purpose, the department must establish a competitive program for awarding grants to municipalities using a labor program model designed for providing jobs to individuals experiencing homelessness to lead to full-time employment and stable housing.
