     (((B))) (ii) The term does not include (((i))) (A) rights to payment evidenced by chattel paper or an instrument, (((ii))) (B) commercial tort claims, (((iii))) (C) deposit accounts, (((iv))) (D) investment property, (((v))) (E) letter-of-credit rights or letters of credit, or (((vi))) (F) rights to payment for money or funds advanced or sold, other than rights arising out of the use of a credit or charge card or information contained on or for use with the card.

     (((ii))) (B) Rent on real property leased by a debtor in connection with its farming operation;

     (8))) (ii) With present intent to adopt or accept a record, to attach to or logically associate with the record an electronic sound, symbol, or process.

     (((9))) (i) "Cash proceeds" means proceeds that are money, checks, deposit accounts, or the like.

     (((C))) (iii) Goods that are the subject of a consignment.
