Specifying conditions for requiring examination of a driver.


    AN ACT Relating to driver's license examinations and restrictions; amending RCW 46.20.305 and 46.63.020; adding a new section to chapter 42.17 RCW; and prescribing penalties.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    Sec. 1.  RCW 46.20.305 and 1999 c 351 s 3 are each amended to read as follows:

    (1) The department, having good cause to believe that a licensed driver is incompetent or otherwise not qualified to be licensed may upon notice require him or her to submit to an examination.

    (a) The director has good cause to believe that a driver is incompetent or unqualified to retain his or her license on the basis of a report by:

    (i) A law enforcement officer;

    (ii) A physician;

    (iii) A physical therapist;

    (iv) A registered nurse;

    (v) A psychiatrist;

    (vi) A psychologist; or

    (vii) A member of the driver's immediate family, who has reached the age of eighteen years, except that no person may report the same family member under this section more than once during a three-month period.

    (b) The report must state that the person reasonably and in good faith believes the driver cannot safely operate a motor vehicle, based upon personal observation or physical evidence that must be described in the report, or the report must be based upon an investigation by a law enforcement officer.  The report must be a written declaration in the form prescribed by the department and must contain the name, address, telephone number, and signature of the person making the report.  The department must review the report and the driver's record to determine whether a driver must submit to an examination.

    (c) A report received by the department under this subsection is exempt from public inspection and copying under chapter 42.17 RCW.  The department shall not divulge the identity of the person making the report without that person's permission, except upon order of a court of competent jurisdiction.  A person who makes a report in good faith may not be held liable in a civil action based upon the contents of the report, the fact that it was made, or the consequences of the report.

    (d) A person who intentionally files a false report under this section is guilty of a misdemeanor and is liable for damages that proximately result.

    (2) The department shall require a driver reported under RCW 46.52.070 (2) and (3) to submit to an examination.  The examination must be completed no later than one hundred twenty days after the accident report required under RCW 46.52.070(2) is received by the department unless the department, at the request of the operator, extends the time for examination.

    (3) The department may in addition to an examination under this section require such person to obtain a certificate showing his or her condition signed by a licensed physician or other proper authority designated by the department.

    (4) Upon the conclusion of an examination under this section the department shall take driver improvement action as may be appropriate and may suspend or revoke the license of such person or permit him or her to retain such license, or may issue a license subject to restrictions as permitted under RCW 46.20.041.  The department may suspend or revoke the license of such person who refuses or neglects to submit to such examination.

    (5) A driver whose condition is temporary in nature may petition the director for total or partial reinstatement of his or her license at the end of the temporary condition.  The request must be made on a form prescribed by the department and accompanied by a statement from a health care provider.  The director must decide the petition within thirty days of receipt of the petition.  A person whose petition for license reinstatement is denied may, within thirty days after the notice of denial has been given, request in writing a formal hearing before the department.  Except as otherwise provided in this section, the hearing is subject to and must be scheduled and conducted in accordance with RCW 46.20.329 and 46.20.332.

    (6) The department may require payment of a fee by a person subject to examination under this section.  The department shall set the fee in an amount that is sufficient to cover the additional cost of administering examinations required by this section.

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  A new section is added to chapter 42.17 RCW to read as follows:

    A report filed with the department of licensing under RCW 46.20.305 that causes the director to commence an inquiry into a person's driving ability is exempt from the disclosure requirements of this chapter.
