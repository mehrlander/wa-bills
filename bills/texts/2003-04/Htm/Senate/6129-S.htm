     AN ACT Relating to membership on the higher education coordinating board; amending RCW 28B.80.400; and reenacting and amending RCW 28B.80.390.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 28B.80.390 and 2002 c 348 s 1 and 2002 c 129 s 1 are each reenacted and amended to read as follows:
     After June 30, 2004, the board shall consist of ((ten)) twelve members((, one of whom shall be a student, who are)) as follows: Five shall be representative of the public, including women and the racial minority community; one shall be a student; one shall be the superintendent of public instruction; one shall represent the two-year system of the state board for community and technical colleges and be appointed by the state board for community and technical colleges; one shall be appointed by the president of the University of Washington and the president of Washington State University to represent the research universities; one shall be appointed through a process developed by the council of presidents to represent the regional universities and The Evergreen State College; one shall be a faculty member in the four-year institutions appointed by the council of faculty representatives; and one shall be a faculty member in the community and technical college system appointed by the state board for community and technical colleges. ((All)) The public representative and student members shall be appointed at large by the governor and approved by the senate.
     Following the term of the chair serving on ((June 13, 2002)) the effective date of this act, the board shall select from its total membership a chair and a vice-chair who shall each serve a one-year term as chair or vice-chair. The chair and vice-chair may serve more than one term if selected to do so by the total membership.

Sec. 2   RCW 28B.80.400 and 2002 c 129 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:
     The members of the board, except ((the chair and)) the student member, who are appointed by the governor shall serve for terms of four years, the terms expiring on June 30th of the fourth year of the term ((except that in the case of initial members, two shall be appointed to two-year terms, three shall be appointed to three-year terms, and three shall be appointed to four-year terms)). The rest of the members serve terms as follows: (1) The student member shall hold his or her office for a term of one year from the first day of July; (2) the superintendent of public instruction's term shall be concurrent with his or her term of office; and (3) the member appointed by the state board for community and technical colleges, the member representing the research universities, the member representing the regional universities and The Evergreen State College, and both faculty members shall serve terms of two years.
