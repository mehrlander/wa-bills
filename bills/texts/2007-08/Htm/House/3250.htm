     AN ACT Relating to restocking fees; and adding a new section to chapter 19.86 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 19.86 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) No retailer may charge a restocking fee if the merchandise that is to be returned or exchanged is defective or damaged.
     (2) As used in this section, "restocking fee" means a fee charged by a retailer for the return or exchange of merchandise.
