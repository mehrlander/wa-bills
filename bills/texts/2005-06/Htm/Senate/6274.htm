     AN ACT Relating to an independent forest and fish science panel; and adding a new section to chapter 76.09 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 76.09 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The independent forest and fish science panel is created.
     (2) The independent forest and fish science panel is composed of the following seven members, appointed by the governor:
     (a) A fish biologist;
     (b) A conservation biologist;
     (c) A geomorphologist;
     (d) A geologist;
     (e) A hydrologist; and
     (f) Two persons with substantial experience assessing adaptive management programs.
     (3) Of the initial members appointed to the independent forest and fish science panel by the governor, two members must serve two-year terms, two members must serve three-year terms, and three members must serve four-year terms. Thereafter, members of the panel serve four- year terms. A member of the panel may serve no more than two full terms. Vacancies on the panel must be filled in the same manner as the original appointments.
     (4) The purpose of the independent forest and fish science panel is to provide advice to the commissioner with respect to the adaptive management program created under this chapter and rules implementing this chapter. The commissioner may consult with the panel and ask the panel to review information produced by the adaptive management program. The panel must be governed by generally accepted guidelines and practices governing activities of independent science boards such as the national academy of sciences. The panel must help ensure that sound science is used in the adaptive management program.
