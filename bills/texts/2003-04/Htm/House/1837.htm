     AN ACT Relating to providing flexibility for fire protection districts; and amending RCW 52.02.020.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 52.02.020 and 1991 c 360 s 10 are each amended to read as follows:
     Fire protection districts for the provision of fire prevention services, fire suppression services, emergency medical and health clinic services, and for the protection of life and property in areas outside of cities and towns, except where the cities and towns have been annexed into a fire protection district or where the district is continuing service pursuant to RCW 35.02.202, are authorized to be established as provided in this title.
