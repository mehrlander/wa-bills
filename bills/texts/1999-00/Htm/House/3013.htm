    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 7.  A new section is added to 1999 c 309 (uncodified) to read as follows:

    With the appropriations in sections 1 through 6 of this act, the state is providing a portion of the local share of the listed political subdivisions' costs for criminal justice, public health, and police and fire services, in support of programs and services that are the ongoing responsibility of the recipient political subdivision.  The appropriations in sections 1 through 6 of this act are transfers of local government costs under RCW 43.135.060(2), but they do not constitute a state obligation after June 30, 2001.
