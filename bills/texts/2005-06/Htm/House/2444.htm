     AN ACT Relating to providing information to the legislature about the performance of state contracts for goods or services; adding a new section to chapter 43.19 RCW; and creating new sections.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that the efficient use of state resources for the public good requires effective oversight of state contracts for goods and services. The legislature further finds that information about the performance of these contracts from those in the private sector who have contracted with the state is necessary for effective oversight of these contracts.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 43.19 RCW to read as follows:
     A contract for the purchase of goods or services entered into with a private sector contractor must contain a clause substantially including the following requirement: The contractor agrees to identify and make reasonably available an employee of the contractor who is knowledgeable about this contract to appear before, and to answer questions of, the Washington state legislature about the performance of this contract's obligations.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act may be known and cited as the taxpayer business contractor act.
