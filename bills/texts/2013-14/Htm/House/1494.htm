     AN ACT Relating to providing additional funding for the state's higher education institutions and their students; amending RCW 83.100.040; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   INTENT. The legislature finds that the per-student level of funding at each of our state's higher education institutions fails to reach the sixtieth percentile of peer level funding as directed in RCW 28B.15.068. The legislature finds further, that steep tuition increases levied over the past several biennia are threatening to place the opportunity for a higher education out of the reach of our state's middle class. Therefore, the legislature intends to provide additional funding for our state's higher education institutions and their students.
