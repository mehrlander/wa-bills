     AN ACT Relating to port districts; and adding a new section to chapter 53.08 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 53.08 RCW to read as follows:
     A port district may receive compensation for consulting services it provides on matters within the scope of this title or Title 14 RCW.
