     AN ACT Relating to requiring that reports be submitted to legislative education committees; creating a new section; and providing an expiration date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   (1) By December 1, 2014, the reports in this section shall be submitted to the legislative education committees.
     (2) The joint audit and review committee shall review all annual compliance reports required of school districts and make recommendations to the legislative education committees regarding reports that should be:
     (a) Discontinued;
     (b) Integrated into the comprehensive education data and research system, which is the longitudinal data system in the office of the superintendent of public instruction; or
     (c) Maintained in their current form.
     (3) The office of the superintendent of public instruction shall report to the legislative education committees on the actions taken in accordance with the federal waiver of the elementary and secondary education act, which requires a reduction in duplication and unnecessary administrative and reporting requirements on school districts by the state, and which of the requirements were:
     (a) Discontinued;
     (b) Integrated into the comprehensive education data and research system, which is the longitudinal data system in the office of the superintendent of public instruction; or
     (c) Maintained in their current form.
     (4) This section expires August 1, 2015.
