AN ACT Relating to the medicaid fraud false claims act; and amending RCW 43.131.419 and 43.131.420.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

The qui tam provisions of the medicaid fraud false claims act as established under chapter 74.66 RCW shall be terminated on June 30, ((2016)) 2023, as provided in RCW 43.131.420.
