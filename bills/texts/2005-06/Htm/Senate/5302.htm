     AN ACT Relating to compensation for school district boards of directors; and adding a new section to chapter 28A.320 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 28A.320 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The school district board of directors may adopt a resolution authorizing each member of the school district board of directors to receive compensation in an amount not to exceed one hundred dollars for each day during which the member attends an official meeting of the group or performs statutorily prescribed duties approved by the chairperson of the board.
     (2) A person shall not receive compensation for a day of service under this section if:
     (a) The person occupies a position normally regarded as full time in nature, in any agency of the federal government, Washington state government, Washington state local government, or school district in Washington state; and
     (b) Receives any compensation from such government for working that day.
