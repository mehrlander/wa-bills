     AN ACT Relating to the taxation of beekeepers; adding new sections to chapter 82.04 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.12 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.04 RCW to read as follows:
     This chapter does not apply to amounts derived from the wholesale sale of bee products by a person who owns or keeps forty-five or more bee colonies used for pollination and is registered under RCW 15.60.021. For the purposes of this section, "bee products" means queen bees, packaged bees, honey, pollen, bee's wax, propolis, or other substances obtained from honey bees. Bee products do not include manufactured substances or articles.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 82.04 RCW to read as follows:
     This chapter does not apply to amounts received by any apiarist registered under RCW 15.60.021 from providing bee pollination services using a bee colony owned or kept by the person providing the pollination services, if the person owns or keeps forty-five or more bee colonies.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 82.08 RCW to read as follows:
     The tax levied by RCW 82.08.020 does not apply to the sale of pollinating bees to an apiarist registered under RCW 15.60.021 if the apiarist owns or keeps forty-five or more bee colonies used for pollination.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4   A new section is added to chapter 82.12 RCW to read as follows:
     The provisions of this chapter do not apply in respect to the use of pollinating bees by an apiarist registered under RCW 15.60.021 if the apiarist owns or keeps forty-five or more bee colonies used for pollination.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 5   This act takes effect July 1, 2008.
