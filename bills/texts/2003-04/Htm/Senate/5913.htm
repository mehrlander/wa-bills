     AN ACT Relating to a master plan for education prekindergarten through university; and creating new sections.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   Research suggests that creating a more integrated, seamless education system involves a host of complex issues, including standards, testing, educator preparation, college admissions policies, governance, and funding streams. Over the past decade, a number of states have begun to move away from dealing with such issues on a piecemeal basis in favor of a more comprehensive approach. Commonly referred to as P-16 or P-20, these comprehensive state programs reflect the central vision of a coherent, flexible continuum of public education that stretches from preschool through the baccalaureate degree or beyond.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2    This act shall be known as the master plan for education act of 2003.
