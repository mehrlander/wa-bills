     AN ACT Relating to economic stimulus transportation funding and appropriations; creating new sections; making appropriations and authorizing capital improvements; and declaring an emergency.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   (1) It is the intent of the governor and the legislature to stimulate Washington's lagging economy and reduce the state's unemployment rate by quickly putting people to work on infrastructure projects that will promote safety, relieve traffic congestion, and make long-term investments that will provide benefits into the future. Such projects must be constructed quickly and generate a significant number of jobs in order to strengthen the economy and assist those seeking work. To that end, eighteen pavement preservation projects with an estimated value of $98 million and transportation improvement projects valued at more than $292 million will be advanced and go to advertisement no later than June 30, 2009.
     (2) This economic stimulus transportation budget is hereby adopted and, subject to the provisions set forth, the amounts specified, or as much thereof as may be necessary to accomplish the purposes designated, are hereby appropriated for the period as set forth in section 305 of this act.

Miscellaneous and Supplemental Provisions

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 301   In sections 101 through 103 of this act, the amounts shown under the headings "Prior Biennia," "Future Biennia," and "Total" are for informational purposes only and do not constitute legislative approval of these amounts. "Prior biennia" typically refers to the immediate prior biennium for reappropriations, but may refer to multiple biennia in the case of specific projects. A "future biennia" amount is an estimate of what may be appropriated for the project or program in the 2009-2011 biennium and the following three biennia; an amount of zero does not necessarily constitute legislative intent to not provide funding for the project or program in the future.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 302   In sections 201 through 203 of this act, the amounts shown under the headings "Prior Biennia," "Future Biennia," and "Total" are for informational purposes only and do not constitute legislative approval of these amounts. "Prior biennia" typically refers to the immediate prior biennium for reappropriations, but may refer to multiple biennia in the case of specific projects. A "future biennia" amount is an estimate of what may be appropriated for the project or program in the 2011-2013 biennium and the following three biennia; an amount of zero does not necessarily constitute legislative intent to not provide funding for the project or program in the future.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 303   "Reappropriations" in this act are appropriations and, unless the context clearly provides otherwise, are subject to the relevant conditions and limitations applicable to appropriations. Reappropriations shall be limited to the unexpended balances remaining on June 30, 2009, from the 2007-2009 biennial appropriations for each project.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 304   This act is necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, or safety, or support of the state government and its existing public institutions, and takes effect immediately.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 305   (1) The appropriations in sections 101 through 103 of this act are for the period ending June 30, 2009.
     (2) The appropriations in sections 201 through 203 of this act are for the period ending June 30, 2011.
