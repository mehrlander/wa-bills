NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that chapter 42.17 RCW contains laws relating to several discrete subjects. Therefore, the purpose of this act is to recodify some of those laws and create a new chapter in the Revised Code of Washington that contains laws pertaining to public records.
