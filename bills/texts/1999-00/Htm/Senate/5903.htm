Authorizing tax, levy, and execution exemptions for properties of Indian housing authorities for low-income housing programs.

    AN ACT Relating to authorizing tax, levy, and execution exemptions for properties of Indian housing authorities designated for low-income housing program uses; adding new sections to chapter 84.36 RCW; and creating a new section.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  The legislature finds there is insufficient affordable housing for low-income persons residing in the state of Washington and that the need for affordable housing is acute in Indian communities within the state.  It is the intent of the legislature to encourage and promote tribal government efforts to develop affordable housing programs for its tribal members of low income and for other persons of low income within their service areas.  This act is intended to provide tax exempt status to properties used by tribal housing authorities and intertribal housing authorities so as to enable such authorities to use funds provided by the federal government under the Native American Housing Assistance and Self Determination Act in compliance with that act.  This act is also intended to accord such properties protection against levies and executions to the same extent such protection is provided to other public housing authorities within the state.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  Unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:

    (1) "Tribal government" means the governing body of a federally recognized Indian tribe.

    (2) "Tribal member" means an enrolled member of a federally recognized Indian tribe.

    (3) "Tribal housing authority" means the tribal government or an agency or branch of the tribal government which operates and administers housing programs for the tribe.

    (4) "Intertribal housing authority" means a housing authority created by a consortium of tribal governments to operate and administer housing programs for and on behalf of such tribes.

    (5) "Indian reservation" means all lands within the exterior boundaries of an Indian reservation and all lands held by the federal government in trust for the tribe and under the jurisdiction of the tribal government.

    (6) "Indian housing service area" means the service area designated and approved by the federal government as the geographical area and service population within which and to whom a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority provides its services.

    (7) "Property" means all property of a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or an intertribal housing authority, including real property held in fee title located within its Indian reservation or Indian housing service area which has been expressly designated for use in tribal low-income housing and personal property used in the operation and administration of tribal housing programs.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3.  Property of a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or an intertribal housing authority is declared to be public property used for essential public and governmental purposes and such property and the tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority shall be exempt from all taxes and special assessments of any city, county, state, or political subdivision where such property is situated.  Where improvements, services, or facilities are furnished by a city, county, or political subdivision for the benefit of a tribal housing project, the tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority may agree to make to the city, county, state, or other political subdivision, reasonable reimbursements of its fair share of the cost of such improvement or facility or reasonable payments for services provided.  In no event shall any reimbursement for costs exceed the amount last levied as an annual tax of such city, county, state, or political subdivision upon the property prior to the time of its acquisition by the tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority nor shall any payments for services exceed payments made by other individual low-income users for the same services.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4.  Real property of a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority shall be exempt from levy and sale by virtue of an execution, and no execution or other judicial process shall issue against the same nor shall any judgment against such authority be a charge or lien upon its real property.  The provisions of this section shall not apply to or limit the right of obligees to foreclose or otherwise enforce the terms of any mortgage of a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority or the right of obligees to pursue any remedies available to it under the law to enforce any pledge or lien given by a tribal government, tribal housing authority, or intertribal housing authority on its rents, fees, or revenues.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 5.  Sections 2 through 4 of this act are added to chapter 84.36 RCW.
