     AN ACT Relating to state employees; and adding a new section to chapter 41.06 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 41.06 RCW to read as follows:
     State employees called to active duty with any branch of the United States military shall return to work with credit for the periodic increment date for that time served on active military duty. For purposes of this section, "periodic increment date" has the same meaning as defined in WAC 357-28-050 as it exists on the effective date of this section.
