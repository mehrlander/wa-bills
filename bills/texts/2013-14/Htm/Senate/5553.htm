     AN ACT Relating to studying the potential to divert ambulances to urgent care; adding a new section to chapter 18.73 RCW; and providing an expiration date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 18.73 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The department shall complete a study of the potential to divert ambulances to urgent care facilities when appropriate. The department may convene a stakeholder group including the ambulance service providers, emergency department providers, urgent care facilities, and insurance carriers. The study must be provided to the health committees of the legislature by October 1, 2013, and must include recommendations on any statutory changes needed.
     (2) This section expires August 1, 2014.
