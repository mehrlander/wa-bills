Increasing penalties for committing assault 4 in the presence of a minor child.

    AN ACT Relating to fourth degree assault committed in the presence of a minor child; amending RCW 9A.36.041; reenacting and amending RCW 9.94A.320; and prescribing penalties.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    Sec. 1.  RCW 9A.36.041 and 1987 c 188 s 2 are each amended to read as follows:

    (1) A person is guilty of assault in the fourth degree if, under circumstances not amounting to assault in the first, second, or third degree, or custodial assault, he or she assaults another.

    (2) Except when subsection (3) of this section applies, assault in the fourth degree is a gross misdemeanor.

    (3) Assault in the fourth degree is a class C felony if the assault takes place in the presence of the person's or the victim's minor child or stepchild, or a minor child residing within the household of the person or victim, and the person is at least eighteen years old.
