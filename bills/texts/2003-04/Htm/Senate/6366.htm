     AN ACT Relating to a study of commercial vehicle parking; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislative transportation committee shall conduct a feasibility study of the potential for local government entities, including but not limited to port districts, to provide locations for parking commercial motor vehicles as defined in RCW 46.04.140, at night.
     The study must, at a minimum:
     (1) Identify the factors that should be examined in determining the best locations for parking commercial motor vehicles at night;
     (2) Apply the identified factors to an analysis of potential locations;
     (3) Recommend the best locations for parking commercial motor vehicles at night that can be provided by local government entities, including but not limited to port districts.
     The entity selected to perform the study through the request for proposals process shall report back to the transportation committees of the legislature by December 1, 2004.
