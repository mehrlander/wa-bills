     AN ACT Relating to sales and use tax exemptions for on-site sewage disposal systems in aquatic rehabilitation zones; adding a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.12 RCW; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 82.08 RCW to read as follows:
     The tax levied by RCW 82.08.020 does not apply to sales of labor and services rendered in respect to construction or installation of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system, tangible personal property incorporated as an ingredient or component of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system, or extended warranties for ingredients or components of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system. For the purposes of this section, "eligible on-site sewage disposal system" means an on-site sewage disposal system constructed or installed in the aquatic rehabilitation zone established in RCW 90.88.010(2) under a permit issued by a local health officer. An exemption under this section is available only when the buyer provides the seller with an exemption certificate in a form and manner prescribed by the department. The seller must retain a copy of the certificate for the seller's files.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 82.12 RCW to read as follows:
     The provisions of this chapter do not apply with respect to the use of labor and services rendered in respect to construction or installation of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system, tangible personal property incorporated as an ingredient or component of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system, or extended warranties for ingredients or components of an eligible on-site sewage disposal system. For the purposes of this section "eligible on-site sewage disposal system" has the meaning provided in section 1 of this act.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act takes effect July 1, 2006.
