     AN ACT Relating to unlawfully hunting while upon the property of another; and amending RCW 77.15.085.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 77.15.085 and 2000 c 107 s 232 are each amended to read as follows:
     Fish and wildlife officers and ex officio fish and wildlife officers may seize without a warrant wildlife, fish, and shellfish they have probable cause to believe have been taken, transported, or possessed in violation of this title ((or)), a rule of the commission or director, or while committing the crime of criminal trespass in the second degree under RCW 9A.52.080.
