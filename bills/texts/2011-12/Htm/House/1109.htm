     AN ACT Relating to legislative review of gray wolf conservation and management; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature must approve or reject the department of fish and wildlife's recommended final environmental impact statement and the gray wolf conservation and management plan as a whole. Legislative action must be in the form of a joint memorial.
     (1) If the legislature rejects the department of fish and wildlife's recommended final environmental impact statement and the gray wolf conservation and management plan, the department must reconvene the wolf working group and resubmit a new environmental impact statement and gray wolf conservation and management plan to the legislature the following legislative session.
     (2) If the legislature approves the department of fish and wildlife's recommended final environmental impact statement and the gray wolf conservation and management plan, the department may send the final environmental impact statement and the gray wolf conservation and management plan to the Washington state fish and wildlife commission for final review and decision.
