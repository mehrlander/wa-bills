     AN ACT Relating to designating the official state peace monument; and adding a new section to chapter 1.20 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 1.20 RCW to read as follows:
     The international peace arch is hereby designated as the official peace monument of the state of Washington.
