_1      AN ACT Relating to nonhigh school district payments; amending

_2  RCW 28A.545.070, 28A.545.090, and 28A.310.300; and adding a new

_3  section to chapter 28A.545 RCW.

     

_4  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_5      NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  A new section is added to chapter 28A.545

_6  RCW to read as follows:

_7      (1) The legislature finds that there is a significant burden

_8  placed on a high school district and a nonhigh school district

_9  when the nonhigh school district cannot pay the maintenance and

10  operating portion of the amount owed to the high school district

11  as required under this chapter.  The inability to pay often is due

12  to levy failures and other related issues.  The legislature intends

13  to establish a dispute resolution process to help facilitate the

14  purposes of this chapter.

15      (2) When a dispute arises between a nonhigh school district and

16  a high school district regarding payments under this chapter,

17  whether it is the amount due or the payment schedule, either

18  district may petition the educational service district to appoint

                               p. 1                       HB 1029

_1  a mediator.  If the nonhigh school district and the high school

_2  district are in different educational service districts, then the

_3  educational service district in which the petitioning school

_4  district is located shall receive the petition.  If the mediator is

_5  unable to bring the districts to agreement within forty-five days

_6  of the appointment of the mediator, the educational service

_7  district shall hold a hearing within thirty days and then adopt an

_8  amount due or a payment plan within thirty days of the hearing.

_9      (3) The cost of mediation under subsection (2) of this section

10  shall be paid by the petitioning school district.

15      Notwithstanding any provision of RCW 28A.545.050 through

16  28A.545.080 to the contrary, any high school district board of

17  directors may elect to assess a nonhigh school district an amount

18  which is less than that otherwise established by the

19  superintendent of public instruction pursuant to RCW 28A.545.070

20  to be due or section 1 of this act.  In the event a high school

21  district elects to do so, it shall notify both the superintendent

27  public instruction pursuant to RCW 28A.545.070 or section 1 of

28  this act.
