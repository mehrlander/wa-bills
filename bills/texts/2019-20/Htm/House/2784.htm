AN ACT Relating to creating sales and use tax exemptions for firearms; adding a new section to chapter 82.08 RCW; adding a new section to chapter 82.12 RCW; and creating a new section.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1. A new section is added to chapter 82.08 RCW to read as follows:
(1) The tax levied by RCW 82.08.020 does not apply to sales of the following specifically identified firearms, or to sales of any other firearm that is permitted to be possessed under federal law:
(a) AK-47 in all forms;
(b) AK-74 in all forms;
(c) Algimec AGM-1 type semi-auto;
(d) American Arms Spectre da Semiautomatic carbine;
