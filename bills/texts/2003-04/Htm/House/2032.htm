     AN ACT Relating to the work plan for instream flow setting through 2010; and adding a new section to chapter 90.54 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 90.54 RCW to read as follows:
     The state adopts as its recommended schedule for establishing instream flows by rule the schedule for setting such flows contained in the December 12, 2002, document prepared by the department entitled "Work Plan for Instream Flow Setting Through 2010."
