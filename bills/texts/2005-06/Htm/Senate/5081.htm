     AN ACT Relating to monitoring of a child's telephone conversations by a parent or guardian; and amending RCW 9.73.080.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 9.73.080 and 2000 c 195 s 3 are each amended to read as follows:
     (1) Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, any person, other than a parent or legal guardian who is monitoring his or her own minor child's telephone conversations, who violates RCW 9.73.030 is guilty of a gross misdemeanor.
     (2) Any person who knowingly alters, erases, or wrongfully discloses any recording in violation of RCW 9.73.090(1)(c) is guilty of a gross misdemeanor.
