     AN ACT Relating to small trailer license fees and trip permits; amending RCW 46.63.020; reenacting and amending RCW 46.16.0621; adding new sections to chapter 46.16 RCW; creating a new section; and prescribing penalties.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 46.16.0621 and 2003 c 1 s 2 and 2002 c 352 s 7 are each reenacted and amended to read as follows:
     (1) License tab fees are required to be $30 per year for motor vehicles, regardless of year, value, make, or model.
     (2) For the purposes of this section, "license tab fees" are defined as the general fees paid annually for licensing motor vehicles and trailers as defined in RCW 46.04.620 and 46.04.623, including cars, sport utility vehicles, motorcycles, and motor homes. Trailers licensed under RCW 46.16.068 or 46.16.085 or as provided for in section 2 of this act and campers licensed under RCW 46.16.505 are not required to pay license tab fees under this section.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 46.16 RCW to read as follows:
     In lieu of the fees provided in RCW 46.16.0621, trailers less than two thousand pounds scale weight and used for personal use on the public highways may be licensed upon the payment of a license fee according to the following weight schedule:

Under 250 pounds$0.00
250 - 1,999 pounds$30.00

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   A new section is added to chapter 46.16 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) The owner of a trailer that under reciprocal agreements with another jurisdiction would be required to obtain a license registration in this state or an unlicensed trailer that would be required to obtain a license registration for operation on public highways of this state may, as an alternative to license registration, operate the trailer under the authority of a trailer trip permit issued by this state.
     (2) Each trailer trip permit authorizes the operation of a single trailer at the maximum legal weight limit for the vehicle for a period of three consecutive days, commencing with the day of first use. No more than three such permits may be used for any one trailer in a period of thirty consecutive days. Every permit must identify, as the department may require, the trailer for which it is issued and must be completed in full and signed by the operator before use of the trailer on the public highways. Correction of data on the permit such as dates, license number, or vehicle identification number invalidates the permit. The trip permit must be displayed in the vehicle pulling the trailer as prescribed by the department.
     (3) Trailers operating under the authority of trip permits are subject to all laws, rules, and regulations affecting the operation of like vehicles in this state.
     (4) Trailer trip permits may be obtained from field offices of the department of transportation, Washington state patrol, department of licensing, or other agents appointed by the department. The fee for each trailer trip permit is three dollars and fifty cents. For each permit issued, a filing fee as provided by RCW 46.01.140 must be charged. The trailer trip permit fee must be deposited to the credit of the motor vehicle fund. These fees are in lieu of all other vehicle license fees and taxes. No exchange, credits, or refunds may be given for trip permits after they have been purchased.
     (5) The department may appoint county auditors or businesses as agents for the purpose of selling trailer trip permits to the public. County auditors or businesses so appointed may retain the filing fee collected for each trip permit to defray expenses incurred in handling and selling the permits.
     (6) A violation of or a failure to comply with any provision of this section is a gross misdemeanor.
     (7) The department of licensing may adopt rules as it deems necessary to administer this section.
