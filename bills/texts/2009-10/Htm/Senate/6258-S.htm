     AN ACT Relating to peak and off-peak electric rates; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The utilities and transportation commission shall study and make recommendations on developing optional time of use electric rates that reflect the costs of serving customers at different time periods, including peak and off-peak periods. The utilities and transportation commission shall submit a report of its findings and recommendations to the appropriate committees of the legislature by January 1, 2012.
