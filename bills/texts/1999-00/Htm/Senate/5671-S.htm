Changing provisions relating to anarchy and sabotage.


    AN ACT Relating to anarchy and sabotage; adding a new section to chapter 9A.52 RCW; repealing RCW 9.05.010, 9.05.020, 9.05.030, 9.05.040, 9.05.050, 9.05.060, 9.05.070, 9.05.080, 9.05.090, 9.05.100, 9.05.110, 9.05.120, 9.05.130, 9.05.140, 9.05.150, and 9.05.160; and prescribing penalties.

 

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1.  A new section is added to chapter 9A.52 RCW to read as follows:

    (1) A person is guilty of aggravated criminal trespass if he or she:

    (a) Knowingly enters or remains unlawfully in or upon the premises of another in a manner that causes an interruption or impairment of the premises owner's or operator's management, operation, or control of any commercial enterprise conducted upon the premises; or

    (b) Knowingly enters or remains unlawfully in or upon the premises of another in a manner that creates a substantial risk to the safety of persons who are lawfully employed in or upon the premises or to any peace officer.

    (2) Aggravated criminal trespass is a class C felony.

 

    NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2.  The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:

    (1) RCW 9.05.010 (Criminal anarchy defined) and 1941 c 215 s 1, 1909 c 249 s 310, & 1903 c 45 s 1;

    (2) RCW 9.05.020 (Advocating criminal anarchy--Penalty) and 1992 c 7 s 1, 1941 c 215 s 2, 1909 c 249 s 311, & 1903 c 45 s 2;

    (3) RCW 9.05.030 (Assemblages of anarchists) and 1992 c 7 s 2, 1909 c 249 s 314, & 1903 c 45 s 4;

    (4) RCW 9.05.040 (Permitting premises to be used for assemblages of anarchists) and 1909 c 249 s 315;

    (5) RCW 9.05.050 (Evidence--Self-incrimination) and 1909 c 249 s 316;

    (6) RCW 9.05.060 (Sabotage defined--Penalty) and 1919 c 173 s 1;

    (7) RCW 9.05.070 (Interference with owner's control) and 1919 c 173 s 2;

    (8) RCW 9.05.080 (Penalty for advocating sabotage) and 1919 c 173 s 3;

    (9) RCW 9.05.090 (Provisions cumulative) and 1919 c 173 s 4;

    (10) RCW 9.05.100 (Displaying emblems of seditious and anarchistic groups) and 1919 c 181 s 1;

    (11) RCW 9.05.110 (Possession of emblems unlawful) and 1919 c 181 s 2;

    (12) RCW 9.05.120 (Penalty) and 1919 c 181 s 3;

    (13) RCW 9.05.130 (Searches and seizures) and 1919 c 181 s 4;

    (14) RCW 9.05.140 (Exceptions) and 1919 c 181 s 5;

    (15) RCW 9.05.150 (Publishing matter inciting breach of peace) and 1909 c 249 s 312; and

    (16) RCW 9.05.160 (Liability of editors and others) and 1909 c 249 s 313 & 1905 c 45 s 3.
