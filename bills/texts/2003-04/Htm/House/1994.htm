     AN ACT Relating to the pesticide ingredient clopyralid; and adding a new section to chapter 70.95 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 70.95 RCW to read as follows:
     If a person supplies materials to a facility or program and the person knows the facility or program is using the type of materials supplied for composting, the person must disclose to the facility or program that the property on which the materials were grown had been treated with a product containing the pesticide ingredient clopyralid if the property had been treated with such a product within the previous two years.
