     AN ACT Relating to liability to the department of labor and industries for premiums, overpayments, and penalties; and creating a new section.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that reducing fraudulent activity by employers, workers, providers, and other persons is a necessary part of achieving excellence in the programs administered by the department of labor and industries. The legislature intends to enhance the authority of the department to address issues involving liability to the department for unpaid premiums, overpayments, and penalties.
