     AN ACT Relating to hauling construction, demolition, and land clearing debris; and adding a new section to chapter 81.77 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 81.77 RCW to read as follows:
     This chapter does not apply to persons registered under chapter 18.27 RCW who haul construction, demolition, and land clearing debris if (1) debris is hauled in containers of not less than ten cubic yards in volume and (2) hauling of construction, demolition, or land clearing debris occurs no more than twenty times per year for each customer.
