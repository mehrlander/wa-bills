     AN ACT Relating to applying a purchasing preference to in-state businesses; adding new sections to chapter 39.26 RCW; and repealing RCW 39.26.260 and 39.26.271.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 39.26 RCW to read as follows:
     (1) In any purchase of goods or services using a competitive bid process, the department must give a ten percent preference to bids from responsible and responsive bidders from in-state businesses. The preference is applied by lowering the dollar amount of the bid by ten percent of the actual bid submitted. If the in-state business is awarded the contract, the contract amount must be based on the original bid. In the event of a tie bid between an in-state and out-of-state business, the contract must be awarded to the in-state business.
     (2) This section does not apply to the following:
     (a) Purchases through community rehabilitation programs under RCW 39.26.230;
     (b) Purchases from inmate work programs under RCW 39.26.250 and 39.26.251;
     (c) Purchases of electronic products meeting environmental performance standards under RCW 39.26.265;
     (d) Purchases of goods and services made with federal funds; and
     (e) Cooperative purchasing under RCW 39.26.060.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 39.26 RCW to read as follows:
     The department of enterprise services must post and regularly update a list of businesses that have a contract for the provision of goods and services. The list must include the beginning date of the contract, the contract amount, and any changes to the contract.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 39.26.260 (Preferences -- In-state procurement) and 1983 c 183 s 1; and
     (2) RCW 39.26.271 (Rules for reciprocity in bidding) and 2011 1st sp.s. c 43 s 241 & 1983 c 183 s 3.
