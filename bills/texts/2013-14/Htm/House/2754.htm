     AN ACT Relating to authorizing the gambling commission to increase fees; and adding a new section to chapter 9.46 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 9.46 RCW to read as follows:
     In accordance with RCW 43.135.055, the commission is authorized to increase fees by up to five percent in fiscal year 2015.
