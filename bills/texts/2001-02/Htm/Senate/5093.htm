_1      AN ACT Relating to unlawful dumping of litter; amending RCW

_2  70.95.240; and prescribing penalties.

     

_3  BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

     

_4      Sec. 1.  RCW 70.95.240 and 2000 c 154 s 3 are each amended to read

_5  as follows:

_6      (1) After the adoption of regulations or ordinances by any

_7  county, city, or jurisdictional board of health providing for the

_8  issuance of permits as provided in RCW 70.95.160, it shall be

_9  unlawful for any person to dump or deposit or permit the dumping

10  or depositing of any solid waste onto or under the surface of the

11  ground or into the waters of this state except at a solid waste

12  disposal site for which there is a valid permit.  This section

13  does not:

14      (a) Prohibit a person from dumping or depositing solid waste

15  resulting from his or her own activities onto or under the surface

16  of ground owned or leased by him or her when such action does not

17  violate statutes or ordinances, or create a nuisance;

18      (b) Apply to a person using a waste-derived soil amendment that

19  has been approved by the department under RCW 70.95.205; or

_1      (c) Apply to the application of commercial fertilizer that has

_2  been registered with the department of agriculture as provided in

_3  RCW 15.54.325, and that is applied in accordance with the

_4  standards established in RCW 15.54.800(3).

_5      (2)(a) It is a class 3 civil infraction as defined in RCW

_6  7.80.120 for a person to litter in an amount less than or equal to

_7  one cubic foot.

_8      (b) It is a class 1 civil infraction as defined in RCW 7.80.120

_9  for a person to litter in an amount greater than one cubic foot in

10  an ((unincorporated)) incorporated area of a county.  Unless

11  suspended or modified by a court, the person shall also pay a

12  litter cleanup fee of twenty-five dollars per cubic foot of

13  litter.  The court may, in addition to or in lieu of part or all

14  of the cleanup fee, order the person to pick up and remove litter

15  from the property, with prior permission of the legal owner or, in

16  the case of public property, of the agency managing the property.

17      (c) It is a misdemeanor for a person to litter in an amount

18  greater than one cubic foot but less than one cubic yard in an

19  unincorporated area of a county.  The person shall also pay a

20  litter cleanup restitution payment equal to twice the actual cost

21  of cleanup, or fifty dollars per cubic foot of litter, whichever

22  is greater.  The court shall distribute one-half of the

23  restitution payment to the landowner and one-half of the

24  restitution payment to the jurisdictional health department

25  investigating the incident.  The court may, in addition to or in

26  lieu of part or all of the cleanup restitution payment, order the

27  person to pick up and remove litter from the property, with prior

28  permission of the legal owner or, in the case of public property,

29  of the agency managing the property.  The court may suspend or

30  modify the litter cleanup restitution payment for a first-time

31  offender under this section, if the person cleans up and properly

32  disposes of the litter.

33      (d) It is a gross misdemeanor for a person to litter in an

34  amount of one cubic yard or more in an unincorporated area of a

35  county.  The person shall also pay a litter cleanup restitution

36  payment equal to twice the actual cost of cleanup, or one hundred

37  dollars per cubic foot of litter, whichever is greater.  The court

38  shall distribute one-half of the restitution payment to the
