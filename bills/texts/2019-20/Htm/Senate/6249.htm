NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2. RCW 74.39A.325 (In-home personal care or respite services—Electronic timekeeping) and 2014 c 40 s 2 & 2009 c 571 s 2 are each repealed.
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3. This act applies prospectively and not retroactively.
NEW SECTION.  Sec. 4. Section 1 of this act takes effect July 1, 2020.
