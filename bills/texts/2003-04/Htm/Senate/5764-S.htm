     AN ACT Relating to fluoridation of public water systems; adding a new section to chapter 43.20 RCW; creating a new section; and declaring an emergency.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The legislature finds that it is in the public interest that prior to the fluoridation of a group A water system as defined in RCW 70.119.020, the customers of the water system or the elected local water system governing body or operator directly responsible to the department of health for utility operations should make the final decision on the addition of fluoride to the water system.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 2   A new section is added to chapter 43.20 RCW to read as follows:
     The addition of fluoridation compounds to water provided by a group A water system as defined in RCW 70.119.020 not adding fluoridation compounds as of the effective date of this act shall be permitted only upon an affirmative vote of:
     (1) The water system's customers; or
     (2) The water system's elected local governing body or operator directly responsible to the department for utility operations.

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 3   This act is necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, or safety, or support of the state government and its existing public institutions, and takes effect immediately.
