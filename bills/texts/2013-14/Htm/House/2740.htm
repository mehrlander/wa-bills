     AN ACT Relating to overwidth, overheight, overweight, and overlegal loads; amending RCW 46.44.0941; and providing an effective date.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 46.44.0941 and 2010 c 161 s 1117 are each amended to read as follows:
     The following fees, in addition to the regular license and tonnage fees, shall be paid for all movements under special permit made upon state highways. All funds collected, except the amount retained by authorized agents of the department as provided in RCW 46.44.096, shall be forwarded to the state treasury and shall be deposited in the motor vehicle fund. Each fiscal biennium, five hundred thousand dollars of the fees collected under this section and deposited in the motor vehicle fund must be utilized by the department of transportation to implement and maintain an automated system that maintains a database of the state's bridge inventory and the associated structural designs, specifications, and weight limits. When issuing a special permit for an overwidth, overweight, overheight, or overlegal load, the route must be checked by the automated system to ensure that the load will not cause damage or risk to the state's bridges. Any remaining fees collected and deposited into the motor vehicle fund under this section must be used for highway preservation:
