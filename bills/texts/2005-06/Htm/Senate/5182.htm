     AN ACT Relating to single burial use of multiple internment space; and adding a new section to chapter 68.05 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 68.05 RCW to read as follows:
     Any cemetery authority offering single burial use of multiple internment space, or any internment whereby a single burial use is offered in a burial plot where multiple internments will be made, must include the following, or a substantially similar disclosure printed on the internment contract in regular sized font before the signature lines:
     Single burial use of a multiple internment space means that more than one casket may be buried in layers in the same burial plot.
