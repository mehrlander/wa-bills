     AN ACT Relating to limitation on actions; and amending RCW 42.17.410.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

Sec. 1   RCW 42.17.410 and 1982 c 147 s 18 are each amended to read as follows:
     Any action brought under the provisions of this chapter must be commenced within ((five)) two years after the date when the violation occurred.
