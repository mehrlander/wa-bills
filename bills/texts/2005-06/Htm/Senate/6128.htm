     AN ACT Relating to certain flights by state officers and state employees; and adding a new section to chapter 42.52 RCW.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   A new section is added to chapter 42.52 RCW to read as follows:
     State officers and state employees may fly with flight instructors conducting training flights to airports not served by regular commercial air service, as such activity by state officers and state employees is presumed to be within the scope of their duties and not to be in violation of the solicitation and receipt of gift provisions in RCW 42.52.140 and 42.52.150.
