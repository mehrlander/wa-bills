     AN ACT Relating to repealing the unfunded state remittance for persons eligible for the federal earned income tax credit; and repealing RCW 82.08.0206 and 82.08.02061.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF WASHINGTON:

NEW SECTION.  Sec. 1   The following acts or parts of acts are each repealed:
     (1) RCW 82.08.0206 (Exemptions -- Working families -- Eligible low-income persons) and 2008 c 325 s 2; and
     (2) RCW 82.08.02061 (Exemptions -- Working families -- Report to legislature) and 2008 c 325 s 3.
